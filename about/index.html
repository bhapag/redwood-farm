A family restaurant first, a party lawn second

Red Wood Farm is a garden family restaurant and party lawn at VPO Daad, on outer Pakhowal Road in Ludhiana. We cook North Indian, tandoor and Indo-Chinese food, 187 dishes in all, and serve it at tables on a lawn lit with fairy lights. We are open every day from 10 AM to 11 PM.

The name

The name is Red Wood Farm, three words, exactly as it is written on the gate. You will find the same name on our arch at the entrance, on the green neon sign and on the small crimson RW monogram we use on our menus and cards.

The place

You come in through a garden gate and a hedge arch. After dark, the path runs through a tunnel of warm white fairy lights over a red carpet, which is the photo most guests take first. Beyond it is the lawn: open grass with dining tables, low lounge seating under string lights, and tables under palm trees whose trunks are wrapped in lights. In the daytime the same lawn takes long wooden tables for lunches and get-togethers.

The kitchen

The menu runs from soups and Indo-Chinese starters to the tandoor, soya champ, platters, sizzlers, a long North Indian main course, breads, rice and desserts. 131 of the 187 dishes are vegetarian, and every dish carries a veg or non-veg mark. Many mains come in half and full portions so a family can share. Prices include GST and are printed on the menu page.

Two dishes are ours alone: the Farm to Table Special Chicken, our house signature chicken, and Atta Chicken, cooked sealed inside wheat-flour dough.

Parties on the lawn

The same lawn hosts birthday parties, kitty parties, small family functions and office lunches, with food from our own kitchen. Past set-ups on the lawn have included balloon arches, a kite-theme backdrop for a daytime party, a mehndi stage and buffet counters. Booking is done on WhatsApp: you send the date, time and number of guests, and we reply with what is possible. See parties and events.

What we do not publish

We keep this website to facts we can stand behind. Guest capacity, per-plate party prices and packages change with the set-up and the date, so we share them on WhatsApp rather than print a number here. We do not have wheelchair access at the moment, and we say so on every page where it matters.
