# Red Wood Farm: UI reference research

Researched 17 November 2026. Two owner references plus 15 sites from Awwwards (Hotel & Restaurant and Food & Drink categories, including Site of the Day winners Agrumea Farm (5 November 2026) and Tengile MalaMala, and Honorable Mentions such as Faith Ibiza, Lingers, Délice, Studenterkilden and MontRural), plus well-known farm-to-table and garden estates. Screenshots are in `research/shots/`, named `site-d-*` for 1440x900 desktop and `site-m-*` for 390x844 mobile, with `hero`, `scroll`, `menu` and `booking` moments where the site has them. Cookie banners were declined where a decline button existed and hidden from the shot where one did not. Nothing was accepted, logged into or submitted.

Palette reminder: near-black #0B0F0C, ivory #F7F4EC, deep garden green #1D3A2B, fairy gold #F2CF83 as an accent only.

## Owner references

### Lando Norris
https://landonorris.com/

Steal: one loud accent (acid lime) used only on the Store button and small arrow chips, so it reads like a signal light; a giant serif-plus-grotesk wordmark pairing (Brier serif over Mona Sans), an intro where a face and helmet assemble from slices, then a free-floating photo collage with tiny uppercase captions ("BARCELONA, 2024") that drifts with scroll; stacked "ON / TRACK" and "OFF / TRACK" headings split serif over bold grotesk; hover swaps between base and alternate images on the helmet cards; a small fixed "Next race" card in the corner that works like a live event ticket.

Avoid: the long blank intro (our first screenshot caught an empty contour background after 6 seconds), 3D and Rive weight that hurts mid-range Android phones, and the nearly white canvas, which would wash out a night venue.

### Disrupt (Made to Disrupt)
https://madetodisrupt.com/

Steal: the huge, tight, all-caps display headline in the middle of a dark field ("BRANDS IN SESSION") with tilted photo cards floating around it; cards that fly in on scroll in 3D depth with the service name set on the image; a glass pill CTA top right ("START A SESSION") that stays put; the copy tone, which runs a single metaphor through the whole page with short, confident lines ("Brand Therapy, Without the couch").

Avoid: Syne and Clash Display (over-used agency fonts), half-empty placeholder sections and client logo slots, a menu hidden behind a popup with no visible links, and particles that add noise without meaning.

## Farms, gardens and estates

### Babylonstoren
https://www.babylonstoren.com/

Steal: the farm told as a place you walk through (Garden, Farm Hotel, Restaurants, each a large image with a short Cormorant heading and a small spaced-caps link); a slim "Make a booking" bar fixed under the hero on mobile; chickens and produce cut out on white as playful section dividers; the line "our menus are always guided by what is available in the garden" as the honest way to explain a changing menu.

Avoid: a thin all-caps light serif that turns grey and weak at small sizes, a promo strip plus a header plus a booking widget stacked above the fold, and the cookie wall that covers the bottom third of the hero.

### The Newt in Somerset
https://www.thenewtinsomerset.com/

Steal: four one-word navigation verbs (Stay, Visit, Taste, Shop) centred around the logo; a hero video inside a rounded inset frame with a hand-lettered seasonal title ("the Winter Keepers"); and best of all, a frosted pill that overlaps the bottom of the video with a live status ("We're closed, see our opening times"), utility links (Book a Table, Get Directions, Plan Your Visit) and a five-day weather strip. That is the right model for an outdoor lawn venue.

Avoid: an autoplay video with no poster frame (a codec-limited browser showed "No supported media sources" in our first pass, so ship MP4 plus a poster image), and a homepage that runs as a long news feed of equal-weight cards.

### Blue Hill Farm
https://www.bluehillfarm.com/

Steal: the plain honesty of Dine, Reserve, Events, Market as the whole navigation, and a hero that is just one dish photo with one white card announcing what is on now ("Family Meal, now serving Sunday lunch").

Avoid: almost everything visual: a dated boxed layout, a "text resize" widget, tiny letter-spaced labels and no motion at all. It is the reminder that a famous farm can still have a weak site.

### Agrumea Farm (Awwwards Site of the Day, 5 November 2026)
https://www.agrumeafarm.it/en

Steal: a pinned hero where product jars rotate in and out on scroll while the page stays still, with a giant curved headline running round a coloured shape behind them; scroll changes the content in place instead of moving the page; a pill "Menu" button with a coloured dot; a warm paper background with one strong brand colour.

Avoid: a pinned section that lasts too long (our scroll test moved the jars but not the page), and the busy label-heavy art direction, which fits a jam brand but not a night garden.

### MontRural
https://mont-rural.emblematica.agency

Steal: a huge editorial serif headline broken across the width with small italic connecting words ("Private Masia / Among the Vineyards / of Penedès"); a drone video starting below a curved notch in the text panel; a split screen where a deep green panel with botanical line drawings holds the copy and the video sits on the right; and a fixed pair of pills bottom right ("Book now" in gold, "Check availability" outlined) that stays on every screen.

Avoid: heavy video blocks stacked back to back, which is a lot of data on Indian mobile networks, and tiny 13px body copy under very large headlines.

## Event venues and party places

### Studenterkilden
https://studenterkilden.dk/en

Steal: the clearest event-venue hero in this set: a short "Since 1854" kicker, a large friendly serif headline listing what you can celebrate ("weddings, birthdays, events, festive gatherings and meetings"), then exactly two buttons (warm "Book a table" and an outlined "View packages"); the same gold "Book a table" button fixed top right on every page, mobile included; and "A historic house with settings for every occasion" as the lead-in to a three-card grid of party types.

Avoid: a plain white page with little motion, and a booking link that leaves for a third-party booking site in another language.

### Faith Ibiza
https://faithibiza.com/

Steal: an arch-shaped mask framing the hero media (a door or tunnel shape that suits our fairy-light tunnel), "MENU" and "BOOK" as two matching pills in the top corners, a floating round WhatsApp button bottom right on every page, and a contact page that puts "Chat on WhatsApp" first, above the phone, email and form.

Avoid: three decorative fonts at once (Dream Orphans, Eastern Harrogate, Afacad), long concierge copy, and 12 videos on the homepage.

### The Rechabite
https://therechabite.com.au/

Steal: the venue name set as a gigantic condensed headline with illustrated mascots tucked into the letters ("MEET ME AT THE RECHABITE"); three equal buttons docked at the bottom of the hero (What's On, Eat & Drink, Book), like a ticket counter, which then snap up under the header and stay there as you scroll; Eat & Drink shown as big photo tiles, one per bar or kitchen, with a one-line description and "Discover"; and a "What's On" programme that treats nights as events.

Avoid: the maximal multicoloured illustration style, which would fight the fairy-light mood, and a hamburger menu of 14 links.

### Délice
https://delice.ca/

Steal: the best menu pattern in this set: icon tabs along the top (À la carte, Pizzas, Kids, Lunch, Drinks, Happy Hour), a sticky left index with item counts per section, and a right column of dish name, one-line description, price and dietary icons, with small and large prices side by side; on the homepage a row of four equal action buttons docked at the bottom of the hero (Visit, Reserve, Order online, Gift card); and an expressive serif headline that wobbles letter by letter.

Avoid: the all-red background on every screen, and dense uppercase in places where sentence case would read faster.

## Restaurants

### Qissa
https://www.qissa.co.uk/

Steal: proof that an Indian restaurant can look premium: a dark navy and gold palette, a big Cormorant name over a dimmed interior photo, two hero buttons (outlined "View menu", gold "Reserve a table"), and "Order" and "Reserve" fixed top right; the "Signature plates" section, where top-down dishes slide along a curve on a starry dark background; large dish cards with small pill tags ("House favourite", "The legend") under the heading "Dishes with a story to tell"; headings like "Explore the menu, course by course" and "A feast worth gathering for"; and a "Begin the story" scroll cue.

Avoid: the gold gradient on every button (keep gold flat and rare), the cherry-blossom blur behind the hero text that hurts contrast, and booking that drops to a generic third-party page.

### Noma
https://noma.dk/

Steal: radical restraint: a full-screen video of the garden and the building, one line of serif text bottom left ("Copenhagen 2026-2027"), and one large outlined "Book a table" button; "Reservations" as the only big navigation word, and a Reckless serif paired with a clean grotesk.

Avoid: an email signup strip right under the hero plus an "Unlock reservations!" pop-up, and a hero that stays black for several seconds in a browser that cannot play the video (always use a poster frame).

### Impasto Forno Antico
https://www.impastofornoantico.com/

Steal: product words as the hero, with no image at all ("FOCACCIA / CIABATTA / SOURDOUGH" in huge type that animates in), a short punchy line next ("From baking bread to breaking bread."), and smooth Lenis scrolling with GSAP reveals over a soft off-white.

Avoid: no visible booking or contact action above the fold, and a menu button as the only navigation.

## Hotels with outstanding motion

### Tengile MalaMala Collection (Awwwards Site of the Day)
https://tengilemalamala.com/

Steal: a calm, wide serif headline set in two offset lines ("UNMATCHED / ABUNDANCE") on a muted solid colour before any photo appears, a single small "Explore Collection" button with a separate arrow box, "MENU" and "ENQUIRE" as plain text in the corners, and a three-photo grid of named places with captions below.

Avoid: a grey opening screen that looks unfinished before the imagery loads, and generic safari stock framing.

### Terreno Barrio Hotel
https://www.terrenohotel.com/

Steal: personality through colour blocking (a pale acid-lime frame around an inset hero photo, then oxblood sections), a script logo with very bold condensed headings, and a rotating circular "Book now" stamp badge pinned top right while you scroll, which is a playful, always-there booking cue; slogans set in a circle around stacked photos.

Avoid: a booking engine bar on top of the hero image, a cookie wall in the centre of the screen, and too many typefaces (four families).

### Lingers
https://www.lingers.it/en

Steal: a full-bleed hero shot of a garden terrace with tables set among tall grasses at golden hour (close to our lawn), a tiny logo, phone and gallery icons, and almost no text; and a date picker with large numbers next to "Enquire" and "Book" buttons sitting over a photo.

Avoid: Cinzel and thin Cormorant everywhere (too wedding-invitation), a privacy modal in the middle of the page, and photos so dimmed that the scene goes grey.

## Patterns to use for Red Wood Farm

- **Hero:** a full-bleed night loop of the fairy-light tunnel (6 to 10 seconds, muted, MP4 plus WebM plus a poster frame, about 2 MB, a still image on slow connections) inside an arched mask that opens to full screen on the first scroll (Faith Ibiza arch, Newt inset frame). Over it, one giant ivory line in a display serif, a gold-dot kicker such as "Garden family restaurant and party lawn", and exactly two buttons: "Book on WhatsApp" (gold) and "See the menu" (outlined), as Studenterkilden and Qissa do.
- **Bold accent, used sparingly:** the neon-green sign is our "Lando lime". Use #F2CF83 gold for primary actions and keep a sign-green glow only for one live signal, such as an "Open tonight" chip or the active menu tab. Never more than one accent per screen.
- **Menu presentation:** the Délice pattern in dark mode: sticky category tabs (Starters, Tandoor, Mains, Breads, Desserts, Drinks, Party Packages) with a short count, dish rows with name, one-line description, price in rupees and small veg or non-veg marks, and no PDF. On the homepage, a Qissa-style "Signature plates" strip of top-down dishes sliding horizontally on scroll, with an image swap on hover for the five best sellers.
- **Booking via WhatsApp:** every booking button opens `wa.me` with a pre-filled message ("Hi Red Wood Farm, I'd like to book a table for [x] on [date]" or "...enquire about a party for [x] guests"). Separate quick actions for Table, Birthday or Party, and Lawn event. Show the phone number as a secondary option, like Faith Ibiza's contact page. No forms to fill.
- **Mobile bottom action bar:** a fixed 3-button bar (Call, WhatsApp, Directions) or 2 pills (gold "Book on WhatsApp", outlined "Menu"), 56px tall, on near-black with a safe-area inset, shown after the hero (Délice and Rechabite docked hero buttons, MontRural fixed pills, Babylonstoren mobile booking bar). On desktop, it collapses into one fixed top-right pill, like the Studenterkilden button or the Terreno stamp.
- **Scroll storytelling:** one pinned chapter that walks from evening to night: the gate and neon sign, the fairy-light tunnel, the string-lit lawn, the table, the party. Use a GSAP ScrollTrigger timeline that changes one image in place (Agrumea) with a short caption in small caps, like Lando's photo labels ("THE TUNNEL, 8 PM"). Keep it to 4 or 5 steps and under two screen heights.
- **Motion vocabulary:** Lenis smooth scroll at a gentle lerp; headline lines rise from a mask, staggered by word; images scale from 1.1 to 1 as they enter; fairy-light gold dots twinkle slowly in the hero only; cards fan in at a slight tilt for the party gallery (Disrupt), kept subtle; hover lifts and swaps images on desktop only; all motion off under `prefers-reduced-motion`. No preloader longer than 1 second.
- **Type pairing ideas:** a display serif with a clean grotesk. Options: Fraunces or Gloock for headlines with Hanken Grotesk or Schibsted Grotesk for body; Instrument Sans also works for body. A premium alternative is PP Editorial New or Reckless with General Sans or Satoshi. Set a serif-over-grotesk split for section titles ("THE / LAWN"), as Lando's "ON / TRACK" does. Banned: Inter, Roboto, Arial, Poppins, Montserrat, and also skip Syne, Clash Display and Cinzel.
- **Copy tone:** short, warm, confident lines with one running idea, as Disrupt uses "therapy". For example, "Dinner under a thousand lights", "Your party, our lawn", "The tunnel is lit. Your table is ready." Use sentence case for body and capitals only for small labels and the hero headline.
- **Sections in order:** Hero, then "Tonight at the farm" (hours and a live chip), Signature plates, the pinned evening-to-night story, Parties and lawn events (three cards: Birthdays, Kitty parties, Weddings and functions), Menu, Gallery, Find us (map and parking), and a final full-bleed night photo with one WhatsApp button.

## Redesign round (17 Nov 2026): five restaurant sites with light and dark section rhythm

Sources: the Awwwards Hotel & Restaurant and Restaurant listings. Each live homepage was captured full-page at 390 and 1440 px with Playwright; the captures are in `report/redesign/research/`. Fonts were judged by eye from the captures, not checked in code.

| Site | Award | What it does | What we borrow |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ballena** (ballenacabo.com, fine dining, Los Cabos) | Honorable Mention, Jun 2026 | Alternates bone-white editorial blocks with full-bleed photos and solid colour panels: a terracotta menu panel and a sage-green contact card. A big caps grotesk carries the headlines. A four-tile grid links Events, Gallery and Gift cards. | **A solid colour panel holding the text beside a photo**, never text on the photo. A colour block as a section (our deep green). The contact and hours card as its own coloured panel. |
| **Studenterkilden** (studenterkilden.dk, inn and event venue, Denmark) | Honorable Mention, Aug 2026 | White page with a centred headline, then a large single photo, then a three-up "offering" row (photo, title, two lines), then a dark charcoal story band, then a full-bleed photo, then the FAQ. "Book a table" plus "View packages" in the hero. | **The large single image with a short caption.** A three-up row for occasions (birthdays, kitty, functions) on white. One quiet dark band for the story. A white-ground FAQ for legibility. |
| **Qissa** (qissa.co.uk, Indian restaurant, UK) | Listed (PRO profile) | Navy and gold throughout, with italic gold accent words in every heading, a dish carousel and stat counters. | **Anti-reference:** the same monotone-dark-plus-italic-accent pattern the owner disliked on our site. Worth keeping: "View menu" and "Reserve a table" side by side in the hero, and category tiles with a photo, a name and one line. |
| **Burrito Madre** (burritomadre.rs, Serbia) | Honorable Mention, Jul 2026 | Cream ground, food photos cut out on a grid, bold colour blocks (orange, deep green) and a deep-green footer. | **Deep green as a real section colour** (the footer and the "Join the crew" card), next to cream. Dishes as a clean grid with names under them. |
| **Pier88 Coast** (pier88coast.com, Egypt) | Listed | Full-bleed sea photo and a script title, then mostly empty white with scroll-driven object animation. On mobile the page is near-empty until animations fire. | **Anti-reference for mobile:** scroll-scrubbed content that shows nothing until JavaScript runs. Our phones get content-first stacks with simple reveals. |

### Applied to Red Wood Farm
1. **Section rhythm:**
   - night (hero, the tunnel)
   - ivory (intro, hours card)
   - deep green (dishes)
   - ivory (occasions)
   - night (a full-width quote band)
   - ivory (visit)
   - green footer
2. **Text always sits on a solid panel**, beside or below the photo (Ballena).
3. **One large single image with a caption** (Studenterkilden) for the tunnel at night.
4. **Dishes:** a swipe carousel on phones with two cards per view, and a grid on desktop (Burrito Madre and Qissa's tiles).
5. **No eyebrow labels and no italic accent words** (the Qissa anti-reference); headlines in plain sentence case.
6. **No scroll-scrubbed content on phones** (the Pier88 anti-reference).
