How Much to Order for a Group: Half and Full Portions Explained

By the Red Wood Farm team. Updated 17 November 2026. 4 min read.

In short

At Red Wood Farm, 24 main-course dishes come in Half and Full; everything else has one price. As general guidance, order one starter or platter and one main for every three or four people, about two breads each, and add rice and raita for larger tables. Takeaway group orders go by phone or WhatsApp.

On the Red Wood Farm menu, only 24 main-course dishes have separate Half and Full prices: the paneer curries, the chicken curries and four mutton dishes. Every other dish, including all the dals, has one price. As general guidance, plan one starter and one main for every three or four people, plus about two breads per person.

Which dishes come in Half and Full?

Half and Full only appear inside Main Course. On the menu page those groups show two price columns, Half on the left and Full on the right.

Paneer dishes with both sizes include:

  • Shahi Paneer: Half ₹225, Full ₹375
  • Kadhai Paneer: Half ₹225, Full ₹375
  • Paneer Butter Masala: Half ₹225, Full ₹345
  • Palak Paneer: Half ₹225, Full ₹295
  • Paneer Lababdar: Half ₹255, Full ₹395

Chicken and mutton dishes with both sizes include:

  • Butter Chicken: Half ₹415, Full ₹695
  • Farm to Table Special Chicken, our house signature: Half ₹415, Full ₹695
  • Kadhai Chicken: Half ₹350, Full ₹600
  • Chicken Curry: Half ₹395, Full ₹665
  • Mutton Rogan Josh: Half ₹345, Full ₹645

Some mains are Full only. Atta Chicken is ₹925, Chicken Handi ₹645, Mutton Handi ₹665, and Malai Kofta ₹275.

When should you pick Half over Full?

Look at the prices side by side. Two Half Shahi Paneer cost ₹450, while one Full costs ₹375. Two Half Butter Chicken cost ₹830, while one Full costs ₹695. For 23 of the 24 dishes, one Full costs less than two Halves. The exception is Shahi Mutton: two Halves at ₹325 come to ₹650, and a Full is ₹695.

So the rule is simple:

  • Order Half when a small table wants to try two or three different curries.
  • Order Full when several people want the same curry.

The menu doesn’t print portion weights. If you need to know how many people a Full serves, ask us before you order.

How much dal makhani per person?

Dal Makhni comes in one size at ₹275. As a rough guide, one order goes round three or four people when there’s also a curry, bread and rice on the table. If dal is the main event at your table, order one for every two or three people. Our Dal Makhani page covers the dish itself.

How should a group share breads?

Breads have one price each, from ₹20 for a Tandoori Roti or Tawa Roti up to ₹80 for a Cheese Naan. Order them in rounds rather than all at once, so each basket reaches the table hot. A good first round is about one bread per person, mixing Butter Naan at ₹45, Garlic Naan at ₹55 and Missi Roti at ₹30. Add more when the first basket empties. Our guide to Indian breads explains each one.

How do you order a group takeaway?

Takeaway is ordered by phone or WhatsApp on 096467 13555. To get it right the first time:

  1. Write each dish as it appears on the menu.
  2. Mark Half or Full for every main that has both.
  3. Give the exact number of each bread.
  4. Give a pickup time between 10 AM and 11 PM.

Get help with a big order

If you’re unsure how much to order, send us your headcount and the mix of veg and non-veg eaters on WhatsApp. We’ll help you trim or add before you place it. Find us at 676, VPO Daad, Pakhowal Road, near Omaxe Flats.

Questions people ask

Is a Full portion double a Half?

The menu doesn't print portion weights, so we can't promise an exact ratio. On price, a Full costs less than two Halves for every dish except Shahi Mutton. Ask us how much a Full serves before a big order.

Does Dal Makhni come in a Half portion?

No. Dal Makhni has one price, ₹275. So do the other dals: Yellow Dal Tadka, Dal Missi Tadka and Dal Handi are ₹245 each.

Can I place a group takeaway order on WhatsApp?

Yes. Send the dish names, Half or Full where it applies, and your pickup time to 096467 13555 on WhatsApp, or call the same number.

Do the sample totals include GST?

Yes. Every menu price already includes GST, so the totals on this page are the sum of menu prices with nothing added for tax.
