Open-air dining in Ludhiana: a garden lawn on Pakhowal Road

By the Red Wood Farm team. Updated 17 November 2026. 4 min read.

In short

Red Wood Farm is an open-air garden restaurant on outer Pakhowal Road, Ludhiana, open every day from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Tables sit on a grass lawn, under palm trees and on low lounge seating beneath string lights, and you walk in through a fairy-light tunnel. Menu prices run from ₹20 to ₹925 with GST included.

Red Wood Farm is an open-air garden restaurant on outer Pakhowal Road, Ludhiana, open every day from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM. You eat at tables on a grass lawn, under palm trees or on low lounge seating beneath string lights. Guests arrive through a walk-through tunnel of fairy lights.

What the open-air space looks like

What follows is drawn from our own photos of the place.

You come in through a garden gate and a green hedge arch with flowers, and past a “Red Wood Farm” name arch. A green neon sign with our name sits on the grounds.

The part guests photograph most is the fairy-light tunnel. It’s a walk-through arch of warm white lights over a red carpet, with green uplights in the trees on either side. A stone garden path runs through the grounds by day. At night, lamp posts light the paths, and the palms along the driveway are wrapped in fairy lights. Trees around the grounds are wrapped in lights too.

The lawn itself has three kinds of seating:

  • Dining tables on the open grass, with the sky overhead
  • Tables under the palm trees
  • Low lounge seating under string lights, with cushions on the ground

In daytime we also set out long wooden tables and lounge seating on the lawn.

One Google reviewer, gurvinder kaur, said the place “felt like close to nature.” On 14 Nov 2026 we had a Google rating of 4.2 from 72 reviews.

When to come: daytime or evening

Both work, and they feel quite different.

Daytime suits a family lunch or a slow weekend meal. You get the green of the lawn and trees, and the long tables make it easy to seat a bigger family together.

Evening is when the lights do the work. In Ludhiana the sun sets before 6 PM through the winter months and after 7 PM in summer. Arrive a little before sunset and you’ll see the tunnel and the palms switch from daylight to fairy lights while you order. We stay open until 11 PM.

A few honest notes on weather. This is an open-air restaurant, so the sky decides a lot. A covered tent dining area with chandeliers has been set up at an event, but we can’t promise cover for an ordinary table. On a day with rain in the forecast, message us on WhatsApp (096467 13555) before you leave home. Punjab winter evenings get cold, so bring a warm layer if you plan to sit out late.

Open-air dining for couples

For two people, the tables under the palms and the lounge seating under the string lights are the coziest spots on the lawn. The lawn is shared with other diners. If you want a particular spot, send the date and time on WhatsApp and ask which tables are free.

A simple dinner for two from the menu:

  • Tandoori Chicken ₹375
  • Kadhai Paneer ₹225 half
  • Two Garlic Naan at ₹55 each
  • Two Rasmalai at ₹45 each

That comes to ₹800, with GST already in the price.

If you both eat veg, try Paneer Tikka ₹375 and Dal Makhni ₹275, with two Butter Naan at ₹45 each and two Gulab Jamun at ₹35 each. That order totals ₹810. For more starters, see our tandoor guide.

The fairy-light tunnel makes a good first stop for a photo of the two of you. Walk through it on the way in, before you sit down to order.

What open-air dining here costs

Our menu prices are published and include GST. They run from ₹20 for a Tandoori Roti up to ₹925 for Atta Chicken, our chicken cooked sealed inside wheat-flour dough. Some everyday reference points:

  • Dal Makhni ₹275
  • Paneer Tikka ₹375
  • Butter Chicken ₹415 half and ₹695 full
  • Farm to Table Special Chicken, our house signature, ₹415 half and ₹695 full
  • Veg Biryani ₹325
  • Paneer Jalebi with Rabri ₹75

Many mains come in half and full portions, which keeps the bill sensible for two or three people.

A garden lawn or a rooftop?

Red Wood Farm is at ground level. We have no rooftop. If you’re comparing open-air places in Ludhiana, the choice is mostly about setting. Rooftop restaurants in the city give you height and a view over the streets. A garden gives you grass underfoot, trees and room to move around. Children have space on the grass, though we don’t have a play area, so keep an eye on them near the paths.

The other difference is distance. We’re on outer Pakhowal Road, near Daad village. By road we’re about 6.8 km from Sarabha Nagar, 6.5 km from Dugri and 6.9 km from Model Town. Without traffic that’s roughly 11 or 12 minutes from each. Evenings are busier, so leave extra time.

Practical tips before you go

  • Book for groups and weekends. Tables are booked on WhatsApp or by phone. Send the date, time and number of guests.
  • Takeaway is there if plans change. Order by phone or WhatsApp and collect it on the way home.
  • Wi-Fi is available if you need to stay connected.
  • Accessibility: we have no wheelchair access. If anyone in your group has limited mobility, ask us on WhatsApp about the walk from the gate to the lawn.
  • Parties: the same lawn hosts birthdays and family functions. See the party page.

Questions people ask

Is Red Wood Farm open for lunch or only in the evening?

Both. We are open every day from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM, so you can eat on the lawn in daylight or after the lights come on.

Is there covered seating if it rains?

Our seating is mainly outdoors on the lawn. A covered tent dining area has been set up at an event, but we can't promise cover for a regular table, so message us on WhatsApp (096467 13555) before you set out on a wet day.

Is Red Wood Farm good for a couple's dinner?

For two people, the tables under the palm trees or the low lounge seating under string lights work well. Send your date and time on WhatsApp and ask which tables are free.

How far is Red Wood Farm from Sarabha Nagar?

About 6.8 km by road, around 11 minutes without traffic, via Pakhowal Road. Leave extra time in the evening rush.
