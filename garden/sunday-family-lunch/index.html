Sunday family lunch in Ludhiana on a garden lawn

By the Red Wood Farm team. Updated 17 November 2026. 4 min read.

In short

Red Wood Farm opens at 10 AM every day, Sunday included, and serves lunch on a daytime garden lawn on outer Pakhowal Road. Many curries come in Half and Full portions, which makes it easy to share across a big table. Large families should book on WhatsApp at 096467 13555 with the date, time and number of guests.

For a Sunday family lunch in Ludhiana, Red Wood Farm opens at 10 AM every day, Sundays included, with tables on a daytime garden lawn on outer Pakhowal Road. Many curries come in Half and Full portions, so a big family can share more dishes. For a large group, book on WhatsApp at 096467 13555.

What time should you arrive for Sunday lunch?

The gate opens at 10 AM, so there are two ways to do a Sunday here.

A late breakfast that turns into lunch. Come around 11 and start with parathas from the sides section: Aloo Paratha with Butter at ₹50, Gobi Paratha with Butter at ₹55, Mix Paratha with Butter at ₹55 and Cheese Paratha with Butter at ₹60. Add Plain Curd at ₹125 and our own herb chutney at ₹75. For the egg eaters, there is a Plain Omelette with 2 Slices at ₹70, a Masala Omelette with 2 Slices at ₹75 and a Cheese Omelette with 2 Slices at ₹85. When the table is ready for more, move on to the tandoor and the curries.

A proper lunch. Come between 1 and 2 PM and order the full spread in one go. We do not publish our busy hours, so the simplest way to avoid a wait is to book.

How do Half and Full portions help a big table?

One way to order for a mixed family table

Treat this as a starting point. Order a first round, then add breads and extras once you see how fast the table eats.

  • To start: Paneer Tikka at ₹375 and Tandoori Chicken at ₹375, or the Tandoori Veg Platter at ₹525 if most of the table is vegetarian.
  • Mains: Dal Makhni at ₹275, a half Shahi Paneer at ₹225, a half Butter Chicken at ₹415, and Rajmah at ₹230 with Jeera Rice at ₹230.
  • Breads: a mix of Butter Naan at ₹45, Tandoori Roti at ₹20 and Laccha Paratha at ₹45.
  • On the side: Boondi Raita at ₹165, Garden Fresh Kachumber at ₹125 and Masala Papad at ₹75.
  • To finish: Gulab Jamun at ₹35, Rasmalai at ₹45 or Paneer Jalebi with Rabri at ₹75.

What is the lawn like in the daytime?

By day the lawn is laid out with long wooden tables and lounge seating, with trees around the edges and a stone path running through the grounds. It suits a long, unhurried family meal where the children can get up between courses and walk on the grass. In the warmer months, ask for a table under the palm trees when you book. In winter, the midday sun is the reason to come for lunch instead of dinner. Bring a light layer anyway, because the shade under the trees cools down quickly once the afternoon moves on. Grandparents who feel the cold may prefer a table in the open sun.

Vegetarian and non-vegetarian at one table

Every dish on our menu carries a veg or non-veg mark, which helps when grandparents eat vegetarian and the cousins want chicken. Vegetarians have plenty beyond paneer: the soya chaap section includes Malai Champ and Afghani Champ at ₹275 each, and Stuffed Champ at ₹300. Our vegetarian guide walks through the whole veg side of the menu.

Booking a Sunday table for a big family

Send three things on WhatsApp to 096467 13555: the date, the time you plan to arrive and the number of guests. We will confirm what we can arrange. A few practical points:

  • Red Wood Farm has no wheelchair access, so plan accordingly if an older relative uses one.
  • If someone cannot make it, takeaway orders go by phone or WhatsApp.
  • If the lunch is really a celebration, such as a roka or an anniversary, our page on small family functions covers how we host those.

To find us, head for VPO Daad on Pakhowal Road. We are at number 676, facing Cerajot Tiles, with Omaxe Flats close by. You can check every price before you come on our menu page.

Questions people ask

Is Red Wood Farm open on Sundays?

Yes. We are open from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM every day of the week, Sunday included.

Can we come for parathas in the late morning?

Yes, we open at 10 AM. The menu has Aloo Paratha with Butter at ₹50, Cheese Paratha with Butter at ₹60 and omelettes with 2 slices from ₹70.

How many people does a Full portion feed?

We do not publish serving counts, because appetites differ. Tell us your group size on WhatsApp and we can suggest how many Halfs or Fulls to order.

Do vegetarians and non-vegetarians eat at the same table?

Yes. Every dish on our menu is marked veg or non-veg, so a mixed family can order with confidence.
