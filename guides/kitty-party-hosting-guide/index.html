How to Host a Kitty Party: The Host's Job, Step by Step

By the Red Wood Farm team. Updated 17 November 2026. 4 min read.

In short

To host a kitty party, book the venue for your turn, send the invitation on the group's WhatsApp with a reply-by date, collect every member's contribution and record it, hand the month's kitty to the member whose turn it is, and keep the food and the games on a simple timetable. The host manages two things: the money and the clock.

Hosting a kitty party comes down to five jobs: book the venue for your month, send the invitation on the group’s WhatsApp with a reply-by date, collect every member’s contribution and write it down, hand the kitty to the right member, and keep lunch and games on time. Get the money and the timetable right and the rest follows.

This page covers the host’s side of it. For picking a place, see our page on kitty party venues in Ludhiana. For ideas to fill the afternoon, see kitty party themes and games.

What does the kitty host actually do?

The host for the month takes on a short list of tasks:

  1. Choose the date and book the venue.
  2. Send the invitation and chase replies.
  3. Pick the theme or dress code, if the group uses one.
  4. Collect contributions and keep the record.
  5. Run the draw or hand over the kitty to the member whose turn it is.
  6. Order the food, or agree the menu with the venue.
  7. Keep time, so the group finishes when people need to leave.

None of it is hard. It just has to happen in the right order, and the host is the one who makes sure it does.

How does a kitty rotation work?

A kitty group has a fixed set of members. Every month, each member puts in the same agreed amount. The full collection, the kitty, goes to one member that month. Over one full cycle, every member receives it once.

Groups decide who receives it in one of two ways:

  • A fixed order, written down at the first meeting.
  • A draw, where the names of members who haven’t received the kitty yet go into a bowl and one is picked each month.

Hosting usually rotates too, but it doesn’t have to match the receiving order. Some groups let the member receiving the kitty host. Others keep a separate hosting list. Whichever you choose, write both lists in the group’s record so nobody has to remember.

How should the host collect and record the money?

This is where groups fall out, so keep it clean.

Use one register or one shared spreadsheet for the whole cycle. For every meeting, record:

  • the date and venue
  • each member’s name and the amount she paid
  • who collected it
  • who received the kitty that month
  • anything still owed

At the end of the meeting, take a photo of the page and post it in the group. That one photo settles most questions before they start. Agree up front what happens if someone pays late or leaves the group mid-cycle, and put that rule at the top of the register.

How to send the kitty invitation on WhatsApp

Send it one to two weeks before the date, and pin it in the group. Keep it to the facts members need. A simple format:

December kitty at Red Wood Farm
Date: [day and date]
Time: 1:00 PM
Place: Red Wood Farm, Pakhowal Road, opposite Cerajot Tiles (map pin below)
Theme: [theme or dress code]
Please reply by [date] so I can confirm the table.
Contribution is due on the day.

Drop the venue’s map pin right under the message. Two days before, send a short reminder to anyone who hasn’t replied. Your final count goes to the venue after that.

How do you plan the menu for a kitty party?

Kitty lunches work best with sharing plates: a few starters in the middle of the table, then one round of mains or a light dessert. Check how many members are vegetarian before you order. What is a good timetable for the afternoon?

Most kitty parties run in the early afternoon. A timetable that works:

  • 1:00 PM: Members arrive. Starters go on the table.
  • 1:30 PM: Collect contributions and fill in the register.
  • 2:00 PM: Games.
  • 2:45 PM: The draw or the handover, then dessert.
  • 3:15 PM: Group photos. Post the register photo in the group.
  • 3:30 PM: Wrap up.

Keep the draw near the end. Members who arrive late still get to see it, and it gives the afternoon a clear finish. If many members collect children from school, move everything earlier.

Questions people ask

How many members does a kitty party usually have?

It varies by group. The member count also sets the cycle length, because each member hosts once and receives the kitty once before the cycle starts again.

Who pays for the food at a kitty party?

Groups decide this at the start. Common rules are that the host pays, that the bill comes out of the month's collection, or that members split it equally. Pick one and write it down.

What happens if a member misses her month?

Most groups agree she still pays her share on time, even if she can't attend. Settle this rule at the first meeting so nobody argues later.
