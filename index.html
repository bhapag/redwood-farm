About Red Wood Farm

Red Wood Farm is a garden family restaurant and party lawn at VPO Daad, on outer Pakhowal Road in Ludhiana. We serve tandoor, North Indian and Indo-Chinese food every day from 10 AM to 11 PM, at tables on a fairy-lit lawn.

Come in through the garden gate and the fairy-light tunnel, and pick a table on the lawn or under the palms. Order from the tandoor while the children run on the grass. Every photo on this page is the real place.
