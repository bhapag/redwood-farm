FAQ

Is Red Wood Farm a restaurant or only a party venue?

Both. Red Wood Farm is a family restaurant open every day from 10 AM to 11 PM for lunch and dinner, and the same lawn hosts birthday parties, kitty parties and small family functions.

Where is Red Wood Farm?

676, VPO Daad, Pakhowal Road, opposite Cerajot Tiles, near Omaxe Flats, Ludhiana, Punjab 142022.

What are the opening hours?

Open daily, 10 AM to 11 PM.

Can I book a birthday or kitty party?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp 96467 13555 with your date, time and number of guests. Party menus and prices are shared on request.

Do you have outdoor seating?

Yes. There is open-air seating on the lawn under fairy lights.

Do you serve vegetarian food?

Yes. The menu has vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes, each marked on this page.
