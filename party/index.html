Red Wood Farm is a garden family restaurant at VPO Daad, opposite Cerajot Tiles. The same lawn where families eat every day can be set up for your party, day or evening.

Birthday parties

Balloons, lights and a table for the cake, with the food the birthday person likes best. Tell us the age, the theme and your guest count, and we plan the set-up with you.

Kitty parties

A relaxed daytime set-up in the garden, from 10 AM, with the full menu to order from.

Family functions

Anniversaries, get-togethers and family celebrations on the lawn, with food from our own kitchen.

Team lunches

Office lunches and team get-togethers in the open air.

How booking works
