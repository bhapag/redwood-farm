Party questions

Can I book Red Wood Farm for a birthday party?

Yes. Red Wood Farm hosts birthday parties on its fairy-lit lawn, for lunch or in the evening. Send the date, time and number of guests on WhatsApp to 096467 13555 and we will share the options.

Do you host kitty parties in Ludhiana?

Yes. Kitty parties are a good fit for the lawn and the restaurant in the daytime, from 10 AM. Message us on WhatsApp with your date and group size to plan the menu.

How many guests can the lawn hold?

It depends on the set-up (seated lunch, buffet or lounge). Tell us your guest count on WhatsApp and we will tell you what works.

What does a party cost? Are there packages?

Prices depend on the menu, the number of guests and the set-up. Ask us on WhatsApp for current per-plate prices and packages; we do not publish them here because they change.

Is the food cooked in your own kitchen?

Yes. Parties are served from the Red Wood Farm kitchen, with dishes from our menu: tandoor, North Indian and Indo-Chinese, veg and non-veg.

What are your hours for parties?

The restaurant is open every day from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Party timings are agreed when you book.

Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

No. Red Wood Farm does not have wheelchair access at the moment. Please call us on 096467 13555 before you plan a visit so we can talk it through.
