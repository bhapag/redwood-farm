An evening at Red Wood Farm means dinner outdoors on a garden lawn on outer Pakhowal Road, under fairy lights and string lights. Come around sunset to see the lights come on, walk in through the light tunnel, take a table on the lawn or under the palms, and order from the tandoor. We are open every day until 11 PM.

## Arriving

Red Wood Farm is at VPO Daad on Pakhowal Road, opposite Cerajot Tiles and near Omaxe Flats. You come in through the garden gate. After dark, the path to the lawn runs through the tunnel of fairy lights on a red carpet; it is the photo most guests take first.

## Where to sit

- **On the open lawn**, if you want the sky and the lights overhead.
- **Under the palm trees**, where the trunks are wrapped in lights. These tables feel more private for a couple or a small family.
- **The lounge corner**, low seating under string lights, for a small group that wants to sit long.

Children have space to move on the grass while the food comes. Please keep an eye on them near the paths.

## What to order on a lawn evening

Starters from the tandoor travel well to a table outside: [Paneer Tikka, Tandoori Chicken and the platters](../../notes/tandoor-what-to-order-first/). Add a dal and a curry, a basket of naan, and Paneer Jalebi with Rabri to finish. The [full menu](../../menu/) has prices for all 187 dishes, with GST included.

## Good to know

- Open every day, 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
- Takeaway is available; order on WhatsApp.
- Wi-Fi is available.
- There is no wheelchair access at the moment.
- For a group or a weekend, message us first: 096467 13555.
