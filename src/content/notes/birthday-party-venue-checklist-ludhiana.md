To choose a birthday party venue in Ludhiana, start with two numbers: how many guests are coming and what you can spend per head. Then compare venues on five things: space for that number, the menu, the time slot, what decoration is allowed, and how easy it is to get there. Use this checklist when you call or message a venue.

## Before you call anyone

- **Guest count.** Children's parties grow. Add a margin for parents who stay.
- **Budget per head.** Food is usually the biggest cost, so work it out per plate.
- **Lunch or evening.** Small children do better at lunch. Evenings suit adult birthdays, and on a lawn the lights do half the decorating.

## Space

- Is the area private for your group, or shared with other diners?
- Is there room for children to move around safely, away from the road and the kitchen?
- Where will the cake table and the photo backdrop go?
- What is the plan if the weather changes on the day?

## Food

- Can you see the menu in writing, with prices?
- Are veg and non-veg dishes clearly marked? At Red Wood Farm every dish on the [menu](../../menu/) is marked.
- Is there something simple for children, such as noodles, fries or paneer tikka?
- Is GST included in the price? Our menu prices include GST.

## Time

- What time does your slot start and end?
- Can you come early to set up the decoration?
- Is there a charge if the party runs late?

## Decoration and cake

- Can you bring your own balloons, cake and décor, or must you use the venue's?
- Is there a power point for lights or a speaker?

## Getting there

- Share the location pin before you book. Red Wood Farm is on Pakhowal Road at VPO Daad, opposite Cerajot Tiles and near Omaxe Flats.
- Check access for older relatives. Red Wood Farm does not have wheelchair access.

## Confirming

- Get the final menu, the price per plate, the slot and the advance in one WhatsApp message.
- Save one contact number for the day.
