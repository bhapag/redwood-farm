A good kitty party venue in Ludhiana gives your group a fixed time slot, enough room to sit together for a few hours, a clear menu with a price per plate, and a simple way to confirm the booking. Before you pay an advance anywhere, ask the nine questions below. They take five minutes on WhatsApp and save most of the arguments later.

## 1. Which time slot do we get, and for how long?

Kitty parties usually run in the late morning or afternoon. Ask what time the slot starts, how long the table or area is yours, and whether you can stay longer if the games run late. At Red Wood Farm the kitchen is open from 10 AM every day, so a morning start works.

## 2. Where exactly will we sit?

Ask to see a photo of the actual area, not the venue's best wedding shot. A lawn, a covered corner and a dining room feel very different for 15 ladies. Our [party page](../../party/) only shows photos taken at Red Wood Farm.

## 3. Is the menu fixed, or can we choose?

Some venues offer a set kitty menu; others let you pick from the full menu. Ask for the menu in writing. If some of your group are vegetarian, check that veg and non-veg dishes are marked. Every dish on the [Red Wood Farm menu](../../menu/) has a veg or non-veg mark.

## 4. What is the price per plate, and what does it include?

Ask whether the price includes GST, welcome drinks, starters, dessert and tea. Ask how the bill is worked out if fewer people come than you booked for. Get the final number on WhatsApp so nobody has to remember a phone call.

## 5. Is there an advance, and is it refundable?

Ask how much advance the venue wants, how you pay it, and what happens if you change the date.

## 6. What if it rains or the weather turns?

For an outdoor lawn this is the most important question. Ask where your group will sit if the weather changes on the day.

## 7. Can we bring our own decoration, cake or games?

Most groups have a theme, a cake or a housie set. Ask what is allowed and whether there is a table for the cake.

## 8. Is the place easy to reach for everyone?

Share the location pin in your group before you book. Red Wood Farm is at VPO Daad on Pakhowal Road, opposite Cerajot Tiles and near Omaxe Flats. Note that we do not have wheelchair access, so check with any guest who needs it.

## 9. Who do we talk to on the day?

Ask for one name and one number. At Red Wood Farm that number is 096467 13555, on call or WhatsApp.
