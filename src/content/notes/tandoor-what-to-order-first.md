If it is your first visit to Red Wood Farm, order from the tandoor first. Get one veg and one non-veg starter for the table, Paneer Tikka (₹375) and Tandoori Chicken (₹375) are the usual picks, add a basket of breads, then choose a dal or curry once the starters arrive. Prices below are from our current menu and include GST.

## Veg from the tandoor

There are eight veg tandoori dishes on the menu:

- **Paneer Tikka** ₹375, or **Paneer Achari Tikka** ₹375 if you like it tangy
- **Mushroom Tikka** ₹395 and **Mushroom Duplex** ₹375
- **Hariyali Tikka** ₹325
- **Roasted Gobi Irani** ₹285
- **Aloo Nazakat** ₹245
- **Seekh Kabab** ₹295 (veg)

Vegetarians should also look at the **Champ** section: ten soya chaap dishes, from **Lemon Champ** (₹230) to **Stuffed Champ** (₹300).

## Non-veg from the tandoor

- **Tandoori Chicken** ₹375
- **Punjabi Murg Tikka** ₹415 and **Bhatti Da Murg** ₹395
- **Chicken Cheese Kabab** ₹375 and **Chicken Kabab** ₹400
- **Tangri Chicken** ₹375 and **Tandoori Bharva Tangri** ₹395
- **Pindi Seekh Kabab** ₹395 and **Shami Kabab** ₹395

## For a group: the platters

If you can't decide, the platters put several tandoor items on one plate: **Tandoori Veg Platter** ₹525, **Tandoori Non-Veg** ₹725 and **Non-Veg Platter** ₹775.

## Breads from the same oven

- **Tandoori Roti** ₹20, **Missi Roti** ₹30
- **Butter Naan** ₹45, **Garlic Naan** ₹55, **Cheese Naan** ₹80
- **Laccha Paratha** ₹45

## Then the main course

With the starters done, most tables add a dal and one curry. **Dal Makhni** is ₹275. Many curries come in half and full portions, which helps a family share: **Butter Chicken** is ₹415 half and ₹695 full, and **Kadhai Paneer** is ₹225 half and ₹375 full. Our house dish is the **Farm to Table Special Chicken** (₹415 half, ₹695 full).

## Finish with

**Paneer Jalebi with Rabri** (₹75) or **Gulab Jamun** (₹35).
