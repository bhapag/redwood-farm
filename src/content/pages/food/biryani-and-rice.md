## Biryani at Red Wood Farm

Biryani is rice cooked with spiced meat or vegetables, usually layered and finished with steam so the flavors soak into every grain. It is a meal in itself, which makes it an easy order when you don't want to share a spread of curries.

- **Veg Biryani, ₹325.** Marked veg.
- **Non-Veg Biryani, ₹375.** Marked non-veg. The menu doesn't name the meat, so ask us on WhatsApp if you want to know before you order.

Biryani works well for a quick lunch on the lawn, and it travels well for takeaway too. Order by phone or WhatsApp and collect it from us at the time you choose.

## What is the difference between pulao and biryani?

Pulao is usually cooked in one pot with lighter spicing, so the rice stays the main flavor. Biryani is spiced more heavily and is often layered, with the meat or vegetables cooked separately first. In practice, pulao is a side for a curry and biryani is a dish on its own.

Our pulaos are named for what goes in:

- **Matar Pulao:** rice with green peas.
- **Veg Pulao:** rice with mixed vegetables.
- **Cheese Pulao:** rice with cheese.
- **Jeera Rice:** rice tempered with cumin seeds, the simplest flavored rice on the list.

Steamed Rice is plain boiled rice with nothing added. At ₹180 it costs the least, and it suits any dish with plenty of gravy, from Kadhi Pakoda to Manchurian Gravy. If you only need rice to carry a curry, pick this one.

## Which rice goes with which dish?

Matching rice to the main dish makes a better plate. Every dish below is on our menu.

### With dal

Jeera Rice (₹230) is the classic match for Dal Makhni (₹275) and Yellow Dal Tadka (₹245). Read more on our [dal makhani page](../dal-makhani/).

### With rajmah and kadhi

Rajmah with rice is one of Punjab's best-loved home lunches. Order Rajmah (₹230) with Steamed Rice (₹180). Kadhi Pakoda (₹230) also goes best over plain Steamed Rice, which soaks up the yogurt gravy.

### With paneer and kofta

A pulao suits a rich gravy. Try Veg Pulao (₹275) or Matar Pulao (₹260) with Shahi Paneer (Half ₹225, Full ₹375) or Malai Kofta (₹275).

### With Indo-Chinese

Chicken Fried Rice (₹320) goes with Chicken Manchurian (₹355). For a vegetarian version, pour Manchurian Gravy (₹260) over Steamed Rice. Our [Indo-Chinese page](../indo-chinese/) lists the rest of those dishes.

### Biryani on its own

Biryani needs only a raita and perhaps a salad. Laccha Onion Salad (₹125) and Masala Papad (₹75) are the easy extras.

With a spicy biryani, Cucumber Raita or Curd Pudina Jeera cools each mouthful. Boondi Raita adds a little crunch. The fruit raitas are sweeter, so some people like them against a hot curry and others find them too sweet with biryani. If you can't decide, Mixed Raita is a safe middle choice.

## Ordering rice for a group or party

Rice dishes are one size, so the question for a group is how many plates to order. A simple start is one rice dish for every one or two gravies on the table, then a second round if the plates empty fast. We haven't published serving sizes, so tell us the number of people and the other dishes on WhatsApp and we'll suggest an amount.
