Butter Chicken at Red Wood Farm costs ₹415 for a Half and ₹695 for a Full, GST included. It is one of 15 chicken curries in the non-veg main course at our garden restaurant on Pakhowal Road, Ludhiana. Prices start at ₹325 for a Half Chicken Malai Methi and top out at ₹925 for Atta Chicken.

## What is butter chicken?

Butter chicken is a Punjabi dish of chicken in a smooth tomato gravy finished with butter and cream. The chicken is usually cooked in a tandoor first, so it brings a little smoke into the sauce. It tends to be mild and slightly sweet, which is why it often ends up as the dish everyone at the table can agree on.

On our menu, Butter Chicken sits at the same price as Chicken Tikka Butter Masala, Palak Chicken and Farm to Table Special Chicken: Half ₹415, Full ₹695. Chicken Tikka Butter Masala is the closest relative by name, with tikka pieces in a butter masala gravy.

## Our two house chicken dishes

### Farm to Table Special Chicken

The menu calls this "our house signature chicken." It costs Half ₹415 or Full ₹695. The menu doesn't spell out the recipe, so if you want to know what goes into it before you order, send us a message.

### Atta Chicken

Atta Chicken is chicken cooked sealed inside wheat-flour dough. The dough case holds the steam and juices in while it cooks. It comes in one size, a Full at ₹925, and it is the highest-priced dish anywhere on our menu. It suits a group that wants one centerpiece dish to share. If you plan your meal around it, message us before you come and we'll tell you how long it takes.

## How the curries differ, going by their names

Most of these names describe the main flavor or the pot. Here is what they usually mean in North Indian cooking.

- **Chicken Curry:** the plain, home-style gravy. A good test of a kitchen.
- **Kadhai Chicken:** cooked in a kadhai, a deep iron wok, usually with onion and capsicum and a thick gravy.
- **Chicken Kali Mirch:** black pepper is the lead spice.
- **Chicken Malai Methi:** cream with fenugreek leaves, which add a slightly bitter, herby note.
- **Palak Chicken:** chicken in a spinach gravy.
- **Lemon Chicken:** lemon gives this one its sharpness.
- **Chicken Handi:** named for the handi, a deep cooking pot.
- **Chicken Tikka Lababdar:** pieces of chicken tikka in a lababdar gravy.

Rara Chicken, Barmi Chicken and Lahori Chicken have names that don't describe the gravy on their own. Ask us and we'll explain each one.

If you like your chicken dry and smoky, skip the curries and look at the Tandoori section. Tandoori Chicken costs ₹375 there. Our [tandoor guide](../tandoor-guide/) covers it with the kebabs.

## What to eat with chicken curry

### Breads

Butter Naan (₹45) and Garlic Naan (₹55) are the natural match for Butter Chicken because they soak up the gravy. Roomali Roti (₹30) is thin and soft, good with drier dishes like Kadhai Chicken. Laccha Paratha (₹45) has flaky layers. All 14 breads are in our [Indian breads guide](../indian-breads/).

### Rice and sides

- Jeera Rice, ₹230
- Steamed Rice, ₹180
- Non-Veg Biryani, ₹375, if you want rice as a dish of its own
- Boondi Raita, ₹165

### Something for the vegetarians at the table

Dal Makhni (₹275) and Paneer Butter Masala (Half ₹225, Full ₹345) work well alongside chicken for a mixed group. Both sit in the vegetarian main course.

If you'd like mutton, the Mutton section starts with Mutton Rogan Josh at Half ₹345 and Full ₹645.

## Should you order a Half or a Full?

We don't publish how many people each portion serves. The price gap is a useful guide: a Full Butter Chicken at ₹695 costs ₹280 more than a Half at ₹415. Two Halves of different curries let a small table try more dishes. For bigger groups, tell us the number of people and we'll suggest a mix.
