## What is paneer jalebi with rabri?

In North Indian sweet shops, paneer jalebi is a jalebi made with paneer worked into the batter. It's fried, soaked in sugar syrup and usually softer and denser than the thin, crisp jalebi sold on the street. Rabri is milk cooked down slowly until it turns thick and sweet. The two go together because the cold, creamy rabri cuts the heat and sugar of the jalebi.

On our menu the dish is listed as Paneer Jalebi with Rabri, at ₹75. If you want to know exactly how our kitchen makes it, ask us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you.

## Which desserts are seasonal?

Two desserts are marked seasonal on the menu:

- **Gajrela, 100 g (seasonal), ₹75.** Gajrela is grated carrot cooked slowly with milk and sugar until it turns soft and deep orange. In Punjab it's a cold-weather sweet. Ours is sold by weight, 100 g a serving.
- **Malpua Rabri (seasonal), ₹65.** Malpua is a small fried pancake soaked in syrup, served here with rabri.

"Seasonal" means the dish isn't guaranteed every day of the year. The fastest way to check is a WhatsApp message on the day.

## How do the ice creams work?

The Ice Creams section is priced per scoop. Order one scoop or several and the bill adds up scoop by scoop.

- Vanilla: ₹50 a scoop
- Strawberry: ₹50 a scoop
- Chocolate: ₹55 a scoop
- Tutti Frutti: ₹55 a scoop
- Ice Cream with Brownie: ₹95

Two scoops of Chocolate come to ₹110. One Vanilla and one Strawberry come to ₹100.

The Ice Cream with Brownie is the one exception on the veg marks. It carries the non-veg mark until we confirm the brownie is eggless. If your family avoids egg, pick one of the four plain flavors instead.

## Are the desserts vegetarian?

All eight items in the Dessert section carry the green veg mark. So do the four plain ice creams: Vanilla, Strawberry, Chocolate and Tutti Frutti. The only sweet with a non-veg mark is Ice Cream with Brownie, and that's a cautious mark while we check the brownie. A strictly vegetarian table can order any dessert on the list except that one. If anyone in your group has a dairy allergy, tell us when you order, because sweets like these are usually made with milk.

## Kheer or Kesar Kheer: what's the difference?

Both are rice cooked slowly in milk and sugar. Kesar Kheer adds saffron, which gives it a pale gold color and a light floral smell. On our menu Kheer is ₹65 and Kesar Kheer is ₹75, so the saffron version costs ₹10 more.

## Which dessert fits which kind of meal?

A few pairings that work, based on how the menu is built:

- **After tandoor and dal.** A heavy meal of kebabs, Dal Makhni and naan sits well with something cold. Rasmalai at ₹45 or a scoop of Vanilla at ₹50 is enough. Our [tandoor guide](../tandoor-guide/) covers what to order before it.
- **For a sweet-tooth table.** Paneer Jalebi with Rabri at ₹75 is the richest choice on the list. Share one if you've eaten a lot.
- **For children.** A single scoop of Strawberry or Chocolate is the simplest order. Our page on [dishes children like](../kids-favourites/) has more ideas.
- **On a winter evening.** If Gajrela is on that day, it's the seasonal choice. Ask before you order.
