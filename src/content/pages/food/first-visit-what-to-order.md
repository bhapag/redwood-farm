For a first visit to Red Wood Farm, build the meal in six steps: a soup or starter, one tandoor plate, Dal Makhni at ₹275, a curry, a round of breads and a dessert. Chicken eaters should make the curry our house Farm to Table Special Chicken, Half ₹415 or Full ₹695. All prices include GST.

## Where should the meal start?

Start light, because the tandoor and curries are filling. Pick one of these:

- **A soup.** Tomato Shorba and Lemon Coriander are ₹145. Cream of Tomato, Sweet Corn and Manchow are ₹155. For chicken eaters, Chicken Hot & Sour is ₹245 and Smoked Chicken is ₹265.
- **An Indo-Chinese starter.** Honey Chilli Potato is ₹255, Cheese Chilli ₹325 and Crispy Honey Chicken ₹375.
- **A salad.** Garden Fresh Kachumber is ₹125, if the table wants something fresh before the grill.

One soup or one starter per two people is usually enough at this stage.

## Which tandoor plate should you share?

Order one plate for the whole table to share. Paneer Tikka is ₹375 and Tandoori Chicken is ₹375. If you're curious about the Champ section, which is soya chaap, Malai Champ at ₹275 is an easy first try.

We've written a separate [tandoor guide](../tandoor-guide/) that compares the kebabs and tikkas in detail, so we won't repeat it here.

## Which dal belongs on the table?

Dal Makhni, at ₹275, is the one to order if you only order one. Dal makhani is traditionally whole black lentils and kidney beans, cooked slowly and finished with butter and cream. If you want a lighter second dal, Yellow Dal Tadka and Dal Missi Tadka are ₹245 each. Our [Dal Makhani page](../dal-makhani/) explains what to expect.

## Which curry should you order on a first visit?

This is where the house dishes come in.

**For non-veg tables:**

- **Farm to Table Special Chicken.** Our house signature chicken. Half ₹415, Full ₹695. If you only try one thing that's ours, make it this.
- **Atta Chicken, ₹925.** Chicken cooked sealed inside wheat-flour dough. It's the priciest dish on the menu and comes in one size, so it makes most sense when the whole table shares it.
- **Mutton Rogan Josh.** Half ₹345, Full ₹645, for a table that prefers mutton.

**For veg tables:**

- **Paneer Lababdar.** Half ₹255, Full ₹395.
- **Malai Kofta, ₹275.**
- **Saag (seasonal), ₹275.** It's marked seasonal, so check that it's on before you set your heart on it.

A Half lets a table of two or three try two curries instead of one. Our [group ordering guide](../group-ordering-guide/) explains when Full is better value.

## Which breads should you mix?

Order a mixed round so everyone tastes a few:

- Butter Naan: ₹45
- Garlic Naan: ₹55
- Laccha Paratha: ₹45
- Missi Roti: ₹30
- Tandoori Roti: ₹20

Add a ₹75 bowl of Farm to Table Special Herbs Chutney on the side. It's our own herb chutney, made in the house, and it goes with the tandoor plate as well as the breads.

## What is the dessert to finish with?

Paneer Jalebi with Rabri, at ₹75, is the dessert to try first. Rasmalai at ₹45 is lighter. For children, a scoop of Vanilla is ₹50. The full list, including the seasonal Gajrela and Malpua Rabri, is on our [desserts page](../desserts/).

## What if your table mixes veg and non-veg eaters?

That's common, and the menu handles it well because every dish carries a veg or non-veg mark. Split the order down the middle. Share one veg starter and one non-veg starter, such as Paneer Tikka and Tandoori Chicken at ₹375 each. Put Dal Makhni in the center, since the whole table can eat it. Then order a Half of one veg curry and a Half of one chicken curry, for example Half Kadhai Paneer at ₹225 and Half Farm to Table Special Chicken at ₹415. Breads and the house herb chutney work for everyone.

## When is a good time for a first visit?

We're open every day from 10 AM to 11 PM. In daytime the lawn is set with long wooden tables and lounge seating. Dinner is when the garden lights come on, including the [fairy-light tunnel](../../garden/fairy-light-tunnel/), so come a little after sunset if you want to see it lit.
