On the Red Wood Farm menu, only 24 main-course dishes have separate Half and Full prices: the paneer curries, the chicken curries and four mutton dishes. Every other dish, including all the dals, has one price. As general guidance, plan one starter and one main for every three or four people, plus about two breads per person.

## Which dishes come in Half and Full?

Half and Full only appear inside Main Course. On the [menu page](../../menu/) those groups show two price columns, Half on the left and Full on the right.

Paneer dishes with both sizes include:

- Shahi Paneer: Half ₹225, Full ₹375
- Kadhai Paneer: Half ₹225, Full ₹375
- Paneer Butter Masala: Half ₹225, Full ₹345
- Palak Paneer: Half ₹225, Full ₹295
- Paneer Lababdar: Half ₹255, Full ₹395

Chicken and mutton dishes with both sizes include:

- Butter Chicken: Half ₹415, Full ₹695
- Farm to Table Special Chicken, our house signature: Half ₹415, Full ₹695
- Kadhai Chicken: Half ₹350, Full ₹600
- Chicken Curry: Half ₹395, Full ₹665
- Mutton Rogan Josh: Half ₹345, Full ₹645

Some mains are Full only. Atta Chicken is ₹925, Chicken Handi ₹645, Mutton Handi ₹665, and Malai Kofta ₹275.

## When should you pick Half over Full?

Look at the prices side by side. Two Half Shahi Paneer cost ₹450, while one Full costs ₹375. Two Half Butter Chicken cost ₹830, while one Full costs ₹695. For 23 of the 24 dishes, one Full costs less than two Halves. The exception is Shahi Mutton: two Halves at ₹325 come to ₹650, and a Full is ₹695.

So the rule is simple:

- **Order Half** when a small table wants to try two or three different curries.
- **Order Full** when several people want the same curry.

The menu doesn't print portion weights. If you need to know how many people a Full serves, ask us before you order.

## How much dal makhani per person?

Dal Makhni comes in one size at ₹275. As a rough guide, one order goes round three or four people when there's also a curry, bread and rice on the table. If dal is the main event at your table, order one for every two or three people. Our [Dal Makhani page](../dal-makhani/) covers the dish itself.

## How should a group share breads?

Breads have one price each, from ₹20 for a Tandoori Roti or Tawa Roti up to ₹80 for a Cheese Naan. Order them in rounds rather than all at once, so each basket reaches the table hot. A good first round is about one bread per person, mixing Butter Naan at ₹45, Garlic Naan at ₹55 and Missi Roti at ₹30. Add more when the first basket empties. Our [guide to Indian breads](../indian-breads/) explains each one.

## How do you order a group takeaway?

Takeaway is ordered by phone or WhatsApp on 096467 13555. To get it right the first time:

1. Write each dish as it appears on the menu.
2. Mark Half or Full for every main that has both.
3. Give the exact number of each bread.
4. Give a pickup time between 10 AM and 11 PM.
