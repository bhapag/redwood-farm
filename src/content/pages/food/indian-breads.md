## Tandoor or tawa: what the names tell you

Two of our breads say how they are cooked right in the name.

**Tandoori Roti (₹20)** comes from the tandoor, a clay oven heated very hot. The dough is pressed onto the inside wall, where it puffs and chars in spots. The result is firm at the edges with a slight chew and a smoky taste.

**Tawa Roti (₹20)** is cooked on a tawa, a flat iron griddle. It is closer to the soft roti most Punjabi families make at home every day, with no char from the oven wall.

The menu doesn't say how the other breads are cooked, so here is what usually happens in North Indian kitchens:

- **Naan** is traditionally a tandoor bread made with refined flour, which makes it softer and puffier than roti.
- **Parathas** are usually cooked on a griddle with butter or oil. "Laccha" refers to the many flaky layers of the Laccha Paratha.
- **Roomali Roti** is a very thin roti. The name compares it to a handkerchief, a reference to how thin and soft it is.
- **Missi Roti** is usually made with gram flour mixed into the dough with spices, which gives it a yellow color and more flavor.

If you want to know exactly how a bread is cooked in our kitchen, ask us. For more on the clay oven, read our [tandoor guide](../tandoor-guide/).

## Which bread goes with which dish?

Pick the bread by how thick the gravy is and how rich the dish already tastes.

### Thick, rich dals and gravies

Dal Makhni (₹275) is heavy with butter, so a bread that scoops well suits it. Butter Naan (₹45) or Laccha Paratha (₹45) are the usual picks. Missi Roti (₹30) gives it a spiced edge. More on that pairing is on our [dal makhani page](../dal-makhani/).

### Butter Chicken and creamy curries

Garlic Naan (₹55) stands up to Butter Chicken (Half ₹415, Full ₹695) and Shahi Paneer (Half ₹225, Full ₹375). Plain Naan (₹25) is the choice if you want the gravy to do the talking. See every chicken gravy on our [chicken curries page](../chicken-curries/).

### Drier dishes

Kadhai Paneer (Half ₹225, Full ₹375) and Kadhai Chicken (Half ₹350, Full ₹600) have a thick, clinging masala. Roomali Roti (₹30) folds around them neatly, and Tandoori Roti (₹20) gives more bite.

### Chickpeas and seasonal greens

Chana Masala (₹275) goes well with Laccha Paratha or Onion Naan (₹55). When Saag (seasonal) is on, at ₹275, Missi Roti or Tandoori Roti are the heartier partners.

### When rice is better

Rajmah (₹230) and Kadhi Pakoda (₹230) are usually eaten with rice in Punjab. Our [biryani and rice page](../biryani-and-rice/) covers those pairings.

## How many breads should you order?

As a rough guide, most adults eat two or three pieces of bread with a curry meal, and children eat fewer. Breads are cheap next to the mains, so it is easy to order a first round and add more once you see how the table is eating. Mixing a few kinds, say Butter Naan, Garlic Naan and Tandoori Roti, lets everyone try something.
