Most children find plenty to eat on the regular Red Wood Farm menu. French Fries cost ₹195, Hakka Noodles ₹255, Paneer Tikka ₹375 and a Butter Naan ₹45, and a scoop of Vanilla ice cream is ₹50. There's no separate children's section, so families order from the main menu and share. Prices include GST.

## Which snacks do children usually reach for first?

Fries and fried snacks are the easy start, and one plate goes round a whole table. From the Appetizers section:

- French Fries: ₹195
- Cheesy Fries: ₹225
- Peri Peri Fries: ₹225 (the peri peri seasoning usually adds heat)
- Cheese Fingers: ₹315
- Cheese Corn Roll: ₹280
- Spring Roll: ₹240
- Veg Cutlet: ₹245

For families who eat chicken, Chicken Popcorn is ₹385 and Crispy Honey Chicken is ₹375. Honey Chilli Potato at ₹255 is sweet and sticky, though "chilli" in the name is a fair warning for younger children.

## Are noodles and fried rice on the menu?

Yes. Hakka Noodles are ₹255 and Chicken Noodles are ₹345. Chicken Fried Rice is ₹320. Indo-Chinese noodles are usually wok-tossed with soy sauce and vegetables, and plenty of children who skip curry will eat a bowl of them. Our [Indo-Chinese page](../indo-chinese/) lists the rest of that side of the menu, including Manchurian and the chilli dishes.

## What from the tandoor works for children?

Grilled food without gravy is often easier for a fussy eater. A few to try:

- **Paneer Tikka, ₹375.** Cubes of paneer grilled in the tandoor. One plate shares well across a table.
- **Aloo Nazakat, ₹245.** Potatoes from the tandoor, one of the lower-priced veg starters.
- **Malai Champ, ₹275.** Soya chaap from the Champ section. Malai in a dish name usually points to a cream-based marinade.
- **Tandoori Chicken, ₹375.** Plain chicken on the bone. Easy to pull apart for a smaller child.
- **Chicken Cheese Kabab, ₹375.** A chicken kebab made with cheese, from the same Tandoori section.

## Which breads, rice and dals keep it simple?

Bread and dal is the safest meal for many children, and breads are the lowest-priced part of the menu.

- Plain Naan: ₹25
- Butter Naan: ₹45
- Cheese Naan: ₹80
- Aloo Paratha with Butter: ₹50
- Cheese Paratha with Butter: ₹60
- Yellow Dal Tadka: ₹245
- Dal Makhni: ₹275
- Jeera Rice: ₹230
- Steamed Rice: ₹180

A bowl of curd helps with anything spicy. Plain Curd is ₹125, Boondi Raita ₹165, Pineapple Raita ₹195 and Mix Fruit Raita ₹200.

## Which dishes sound mild on the menu?

The menu doesn't print spice levels, so we can only point to names. In North Indian cooking, words like malai, shahi, korma and butter usually mean a creamier gravy. Words like chilli, achari, kali mirch and mirchi usually mean more heat.

Dishes whose names suggest a gentler gravy:

- Malai Kofta: ₹275
- Shahi Paneer: Half ₹225, Full ₹375
- Paneer Korma: ₹375
- Paneer Butter Masala: Half ₹225, Full ₹345
- Butter Chicken: Half ₹415, Full ₹695
- Chicken Malai Methi: Half ₹325, Full ₹625

Treat this list as a starting point. Ask when you order and we'll tell you how a dish is cooked that day.

Ice cream is priced per scoop. Vanilla and Strawberry are ₹50 a scoop. Chocolate and Tutti Frutti are ₹55. Ice Cream with Brownie is ₹95, but it carries the non-veg mark until we confirm the brownie is eggless. Gulab Jamun, at ₹35, is the lowest-priced dessert. ## How do you keep a meal with children easy?

A few habits help at any family table:

- Order one snack, such as fries or noodles, as soon as you sit down. Hungry children wait better with something on the table.
- Ask for breads in two rounds, so the second basket arrives warm when the curries do.
- Pick one or two shared mains instead of a separate dish for each child.
- Keep dessert as the reward at the end. A ₹50 scoop goes a long way.
- Bring wipes. Tandoor food is eaten with hands.

## What should parents know about the space?

Red Wood Farm is a garden restaurant, with dining tables out on the open grass lawn and under the palms. There's open grass near the tables, which gives restless children somewhere to walk between courses. We don't have a play area, a kids' zone or rides, so bring whatever keeps your children busy.

There's no wheelchair access. Plan for that if you're bringing a pram or a grandparent who uses a wheelchair. For ideas on a full day out with children, see our page on [family outings with kids](../../garden/family-outing-with-kids/).
