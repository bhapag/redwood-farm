Every dish on the Red Wood Farm menu carries one of two marks. A green square with a dot means veg. A reddish-brown square with a triangle means non-veg. All prices are in rupees and already include GST, so no tax is added on top. Some mains also show separate Half and Full prices.

## What do the veg and non-veg marks mean?

The marks follow the green-and-brown idea most Indian diners already know from food packets:

- **Veg:** a green square outline with a green dot in the middle.
- **Non-veg:** a reddish-brown square outline with a triangle inside.

Of our 187 dishes, 131 carry the veg mark and 56 carry the non-veg mark. On the [menu page](../../menu/) the mark sits just to the left of each dish name. Screen readers announce it as "Veg" or "Non-veg".

Egg counts as non-veg on our menu. The Plain Omelette with 2 Slices (₹70), the Masala Omelette with 2 Slices (₹75) and the Cheese Omelette with 2 Slices (₹85) all carry the non-veg mark. If your family is strictly vegetarian, our [vegetarian guide](../vegetarian-guide/) picks out what to order.

## Why is Ice Cream with Brownie marked non-veg?

Brownies are often made with egg. Until we've confirmed that the brownie we serve is eggless, Ice Cream with Brownie (₹95) stays marked non-veg. We'd rather mark one dish too cautiously than let an egg-free family order it by mistake. The four plain ice creams are all marked veg.

## Do the prices include GST?

Yes. Every price on the menu includes GST. A Butter Naan listed at ₹45 is ₹45, and a Dal Makhni listed at ₹275 is ₹275. When you add up an order from the menu, the sum is the total for those dishes with GST already inside.

The full range runs from ₹20 for a Tandoori Roti or a Tawa Roti up to ₹925 for Atta Chicken, our chicken cooked sealed inside wheat-flour dough.

## How do the Half and Full columns work?

Only 24 dishes have two sizes, all in Main Course. On the menu page, any group that contains them shows a small "Half / Full" heading on the right. Each dish in that group then has two prices:

- Shahi Paneer: Half ₹225, Full ₹375
- Chicken Curry: Half ₹395, Full ₹665
- Mutton Rogan Josh: Half ₹345, Full ₹645

When a dish in the same group has only one size, the Half column is blank and the single price sits under Full. Malai Kofta (₹275) and Chicken Handi (₹645) are examples. Every dish outside Main Course has one price. Dal Makhni, for instance, is just ₹275.

To plan how many Halves or Fulls a table needs, read our [group ordering guide](../group-ordering-guide/).

## Why are ice creams priced per scoop?

The Ice Creams section has a note that says "Per scoop". Vanilla and Strawberry are ₹50 a scoop. Chocolate and Tutti Frutti are ₹55. Order three scoops of Vanilla and the line on your order is ₹150. Our [desserts page](../desserts/) has the rest of the sweet side.

## How do you use the search and filter on the menu page?

The menu page has a strip of tools that stays at the top of the screen as you scroll:

1. **Search box.** Type part of a dish name, such as "paneer", "tikka" or "naan". The list narrows as you type.
2. **Diet buttons.** Tap All, Veg or Non-veg. The buttons work together with the search, so "Veg" plus "tikka" shows only veg tikkas.
3. **Category links.** Tap a section, such as Champ or Breads, to jump straight to it.

A line above the dishes shows how many match, for example "12 of 187 dishes". If nothing matches, you'll see a note suggesting another word.

A few search tips:

- Search uses the spellings on our menu. Dal makhani is spelled "Dal Makhni", and soya chaap is listed under "Champ".
- Search matches dish names only, not the short notes under some dishes.
- Short fragments work well. "makh" finds Dal Makhni, and "chick" finds every chicken dish.
