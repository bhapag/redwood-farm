Red Wood Farm on Pakhowal Road has 16 dishes with paneer in the name. Two are tandoor starters at ₹375. Twelve are main-course gravies priced from ₹295 to ₹395 for a Full, and six of those also come as a Half from ₹225. Paneer Paratha (₹55) and Paneer Jalebi with Rabri (₹75) complete the list. Prices include GST.

## Paneer starters from the tandoor

The Tandoori section has two paneer starters, and they cost the same.

- **Paneer Tikka, ₹375.** Cubes of paneer marinated and cooked in the tandoor, the clay oven, which gives them charred edges.
- **Paneer Achari Tikka, ₹375.** The same idea with achari spices, the flavors used in Indian pickles. Expect more tang and heat.

Both come in one size. Order them first and share them while the mains are cooked. If you like food from the clay oven, our [tandoor guide](../tandoor-guide/) covers the rest of that section, including the veg and non-veg kebabs.

## How do the paneer gravies differ?

The menu gives no recipes, so here is what each name usually means in North Indian cooking. Ask us if you want the details of how our kitchen makes a dish.

### Named for a vegetable or a flavor

- **Palak Paneer:** paneer in a spinach gravy. It is usually milder and lighter than the cream-based dishes.
- **Paneer Do Pyaza:** "do pyaza" points to onions used twice, typically cooked into the gravy and added again in larger pieces.
- **Lemon Paneer:** the name points to lemon, so expect a sharper, tangier dish.

### Named for a cooking pot

- **Kadhai Paneer:** cooked in a kadhai, the deep iron wok of Punjabi kitchens. It is usually made with onion and capsicum and has a thicker, drier gravy.
- **Matka Paneer:** named for the matka, a clay pot.

### Rich and creamy by name

- **Shahi Paneer:** "shahi" means royal, and the dish is usually a rich, cream-based gravy.
- **Paneer Korma:** korma is generally a mild, creamy gravy.
- **Paneer Butter Masala:** a tomato and butter gravy, often a little sweet. It is usually the safest order for someone who doesn't like much heat.

### Other styles

- **Paneer Bhurji:** crumbled paneer cooked with spices, closer to a dry scramble than a curry. At ₹395 it is one of the highest-priced paneer dishes.
- **Chilli Paneer:** the Indo-Chinese dish of paneer tossed in a chilli sauce. Our [Indo-Chinese page](../indo-chinese/) lists the rest of that side of the menu.

### Names that don't explain themselves

Paneer Lababdar and Lahori Paneer sit at the top of the paneer price list, at ₹395 for a Full. Lahori refers to the city of Lahore, but neither name tells you much about the gravy. Ask us and we'll describe them.

## Paneer outside the main course

Paneer turns up in two more places on the menu.

- **Paneer Paratha, ₹55,** in the Breads section. A stuffed flatbread that works as a meal with Plain Curd (₹125) or a pickle.
- **Paneer Jalebi with Rabri, ₹75,** in the Dessert section. It is a sweet made with paneer and served with rabri, a thickened sweet milk. See our [desserts page](../desserts/) for the other sweets.

## What to order for a paneer-loving table

For two or three people, a starter plus two Half gravies keeps the table varied. One example:

1. Paneer Tikka, ₹375
2. Paneer Butter Masala, Half ₹225
3. Kadhai Paneer, Half ₹225
4. Butter Naan, ₹45 each, or Garlic Naan, ₹55 each

Add Jeera Rice (₹230) if your group eats rice. If some of you want a break from paneer, Dal Makhni (₹275) and Malai Kofta (₹275) are in the same vegetarian main course. The [vegetarian guide](../vegetarian-guide/) covers the full meat-free menu.

Two dishes on the menu say cheese in the name: Cheese Chilli (₹325) in the appetizers and Cheese Tomato (Half ₹200, Full ₹375) in the main course. We haven't counted them here because the menu doesn't say they are made with paneer.
