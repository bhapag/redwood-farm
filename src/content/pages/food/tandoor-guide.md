## What makes a good tandoori chicken?

Tandoori food takes its name from the tandoor, the tall clay oven used across Punjab. The oven runs very hot, so meat and paneer cook fast and pick up dark, charred edges. Tandoori chicken is usually marinated in yogurt and spices before it goes in, which gives it the red color most people picture.

Which tandoori chicken is best comes down to taste. Some people want more char, some want more spice. A few things are worth checking at any restaurant: the chicken should be cooked through, the outside should have real color, and it should come to the table hot.

We won't tell you ours is the best on the road. What we can share is that Red Wood Farm held a Google rating of 4.2 from 72 reviews on 14 Nov 2026, and one reviewer, Gurveer Singh, wrote that the "food was absolutely delicious, fresh, and full of flavor." The fairest test is a plate of your own.

## How to order tandoori food for a table

Tandoori dishes work best as the first course. A simple way to order for a family of four:

1. Pick one veg and one non-veg tandoori dish. Paneer Tikka and Tandoori Chicken are ₹375 each.
2. Add a dal or a curry for the main course. **Dal Makhni** is ₹275. Many curries come in half and full portions, such as **Butter Chicken** at ₹415 half and ₹695 full.
3. Order one bread per person to start, such as four Butter Naan at ₹45 each.
4. Ask for more bread when the curries arrive.

That first order comes to ₹1,205 with GST already included. For six or more people, swap the two single dishes for a platter and add a second curry.

For a bigger group, such as a birthday or a family dinner of fifteen, message us first with the date, time and number of guests so the kitchen knows what's coming.
