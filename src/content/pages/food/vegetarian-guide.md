Red Wood Farm on Pakhowal Road, Ludhiana, is a garden restaurant with 131 vegetarian dishes out of 187 on its menu, each with a green veg mark. That covers soups from ₹145, eight tandoori dishes, ten soya champ dishes, 28 veg mains, breads from ₹20 and desserts from ₹35.

We serve non-veg food too, so this is a mixed restaurant. If a pure-veg kitchen matters to your family, ask us on WhatsApp (096467 13555) before you come. Everything below is taken from our current menu, and all prices include GST.

## What the green mark means

Every dish on the [Red Wood Farm menu](../../menu/) carries a mark. Green means veg. The other mark means non-veg.

Two cases are worth knowing:

- **Eggs.** The three omelettes in the Sides section are marked non-veg.
- **Ice Cream with Brownie** (₹95) is marked non-veg for now. We haven't yet confirmed whether the brownie contains egg, so we keep the non-veg mark until we know. The four plain ice creams are marked veg.

Watch out for similar names too. The veg **Seekh Kabab** (₹295) and the non-veg **Pindi Seekh Kabab** (₹395) sit in the same section. Check the mark before you order.

## Veg tandoori and soya champ

The eight veg tandoori dishes run from **Aloo Nazakat** at ₹245 to **Mushroom Tikka** at ₹395. **Paneer Tikka** and **Paneer Achari Tikka** are ₹375 each, and **Hariyali Tikka** is ₹325. Our [tandoor guide](../tandoor-guide/) lists all of them alongside the breads.

The Champ section is ten soya chaap dishes, all veg. Prices go from **Lemon Champ** at ₹230 up to **Stuffed Champ** at ₹300. In between you'll find **Garlic Champ** ₹240, **Achari Champ** ₹245, **Hariyali Champ** ₹265 and **Tawa Masala Champ** ₹275.

For a group, the **Tandoori Veg Platter** is ₹525 and the **Chinese Veg Platter** is ₹575. The menu doesn't list what goes on each one, so ask your server. The **Veg Sizzler** is ₹395.

## Breads, rice, salads and raita

All 14 breads are veg, from **Tandoori Roti** and **Tawa Roti** at ₹20 to **Cheese Naan** at ₹80. **Butter Naan** is ₹45 and **Garlic Naan** ₹55.

The Sides section adds stuffed parathas with butter: **Aloo Paratha with Butter** ₹50, **Gobi Paratha with Butter** ₹55 and **Cheese Paratha with Butter** ₹60. Our own **Farm to Table Special Herbs Chutney** is ₹75.

Six rice dishes are veg: **Steamed Rice** ₹180, **Jeera Rice** ₹230, **Matar Pulao** ₹260, **Veg Pulao** ₹275, **Cheese Pulao** ₹280 and **Veg Biryani** ₹325. Salads run ₹125 to ₹155, and the raitas from **Plain Curd** at ₹125 to **Mix Fruit Raita** at ₹200.

## Veg desserts

All eight desserts are veg. **Gulab Jamun** is ₹35, **Rasmalai** ₹45 and **Raj Bhog** ₹55. **Paneer Jalebi with Rabri** and **Kesar Kheer** are ₹75 each, and plain **Kheer** is ₹65. In season, look for **Gajrela, 100 g** at ₹75 and **Malpua Rabri** at ₹65.

Ice cream is priced per scoop: **Vanilla** and **Strawberry** ₹50, **Chocolate** and **Tutti Frutti** ₹55.

## A sample veg dinner for four

Here is one way a family of four could order:

1. One tandoori starter and one Indo-Chinese starter: **Paneer Tikka** ₹375 and **Honey Chilli Potato** ₹255.
2. **Dal Makhni** ₹275 and a full **Kadhai Paneer** ₹375.
3. Four **Butter Naan** at ₹45 each, and one **Jeera Rice** ₹230.
4. Four **Gulab Jamun** at ₹35 each.

That comes to ₹1,830, GST included. Order more bread as you go.
