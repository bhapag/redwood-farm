Dinner on the lawn at Red Wood Farm begins at the gate on outer Pakhowal Road and leads through a tunnel of warm white fairy lights to tables on open grass, under lit palms or in a lounge corner. Order from the tandoor, take your time over the meal, and keep in mind that we close at 11 PM.

## Arriving after dark

You will find us at 676, VPO Daad, on Pakhowal Road, across from Cerajot Tiles and close to Omaxe Flats. The first thing you notice from the driveway is the palms, each one wrapped in fairy lights, and the green neon sign that spells out RED WOOD FARM.

Past the garden gate and the green hedge arch with its flowers, the path takes you into the tunnel. You walk along a red carpet with a canopy of warm white bulbs overhead, while green uplights pick out the trees around you. It is the spot guests photograph most, so give yourself a minute here before you go and find a table. We have a whole page on the [fairy-light tunnel](../fairy-light-tunnel/) if you want to plan a picture.

Beyond the tunnel, lit paths with lamp posts lead out to the lawn. Trees wrapped in lights mark the edges of the grass, so even the far tables have a glow around them.

## Picking your spot

The lawn has three kinds of seating, and each suits a different kind of evening.

- **Tables on the open lawn.** Pick these when a family wants room to spread out and a full view of the lights. Children can step onto the grass between courses.
- **Tables under the palms.** Each trunk glows with fairy lights, and these tables feel more tucked away from the middle of the lawn. They suit two people celebrating something, or parents with one or two children.
- **Lounge seating under string lights.** The seats sit low, and the corner works best for friends who plan to keep talking long after the plates are cleared.

If you have a preference, mention it when you book, and we will tell you what is free that evening. All of it is outdoors. For a broader look at eating outside in the city, read our guide to [open-air dining in Ludhiana](../open-air-dining-ludhiana/).

## What to order for a table outside

Start with the tandoor, because those plates travel well to an outdoor table and are easy to share. Paneer Tikka and Tandoori Chicken are ₹375 each. Soya chaap fans should try the Afghani Champ at ₹275. For four or more, a platter gets everyone eating at once: the Chinese Veg Platter is ₹575 and the Non-Veg Platter is ₹775. Our [tandoor guide](../../food/tandoor-guide/) has the full list.

For the main course, try our house signature first. Farm to Table Special Chicken costs ₹415 for a half and ₹695 for a full. Vegetarians can share Dal Makhni at ₹275 and Paneer Lababdar at ₹255 for a half or ₹395 for a full. If the table is up for something different, Atta Chicken at ₹925 is chicken cooked sealed inside wheat-flour dough. Ask how long it takes when you order.

Garlic Naan at ₹55 and a bowl of our own herb chutney at ₹75 round it out. To finish, Paneer Jalebi with Rabri and Kesar Kheer are ₹75 each. Every price includes GST, and the [full menu](../../menu/) lists all 187 dishes with veg and non-veg marks.

## What guests have said about the evenings

On Google, Red Wood Farm showed a 4.2 rating from 72 reviews on 14 November 2026. Two short lines from those reviews describe the place well. Isha wrote: "Very good place awesome decor yummy food". Sanjeev Kumar called it a "Crazy location for casual parties and events..staff is also friendly".

## Good to know before you go

- **Hours:** 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM, every day.
- **Booking:** message the date, time and number of guests to 096467 13555 on WhatsApp, or call the same number.
- **Takeaway:** order by phone or WhatsApp and collect.
- **Wi-Fi:** available.
- **Access:** there is no wheelchair access.
- **Cold months:** December and January nights get cold on an open lawn. Our page on [winter evenings on the lawn](../winter-evenings-on-the-lawn/) covers what to wear and what to order.

## The same lawn by daylight

If an evening visit goes well, the lawn is worth seeing in daylight too. We open at 10 AM, and by day the grass is set with long wooden tables and lounge seating, with the stone garden path winding through the grounds. A daytime meal feels quite different from a dinner under the lights, and it is worth trying both.

## When an evening turns into a party

The same lawn hosts birthdays, kitty parties and family functions, with set-ups that have included balloon arches, a mehndi stage and buffet counters on the grass. If you are thinking of booking one, start on our [party page](../../party/).
