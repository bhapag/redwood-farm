For a date night in Ludhiana with an outdoor feel, book an evening table on our garden lawn on outer Pakhowal Road. The palms and paths are lit with fairy lights after sunset, the fairy-light tunnel is a two-minute walk from your table, and the kitchen serves tandoor, North Indian and Indo-Chinese dishes until 11:00 PM.

## Why couples pick a garden over a mall food court

Most date ideas in Ludhiana end up indoors: a mall, a cafe in a market, a cinema. Our lawn gives you open sky and soft light instead. The light comes from what is already in the garden:

- palms along the driveway wrapped in fairy lights
- trees wrapped in lights near the tables
- lamp posts along the stone garden paths
- string lights over the low lounge seating
- the green neon RED WOOD FARM sign near the entrance

It is quieter than a city-centre restaurant on most weeknights, and you are not rushed to give up the table.

## What to know before you book

Red Wood Farm is a family restaurant first. Families eat at the next table, and on some evenings a party is booked on part of the lawn. We don't have private cabins, closed rooms or a candle set-up. If a calm evening matters, message us at 096467 13555 and ask whether anything is booked on your date, then pick the night that suits you.

The tables are outdoors. From November to February, Ludhiana evenings on an open lawn turn cold after dark, so bring a jacket or a shawl even if the afternoon was warm. In the summer months, a later booking is cooler.

## A simple plan for the evening

**Arrive just before sunset.** You see the lights come on one string at a time, and the garden paths are easy to walk while there is still some daylight.

**Walk the tunnel first.** The fairy-light tunnel over the red carpet is where most guests take photos. It is less busy early in the evening. Our [tunnel page](../fairy-light-tunnel/) has a few tips for photos on a phone.

**Pick your table.** A round table beside an uplit palm feels a little set apart from the rest of the lawn. The low lounge seating under string lights suits a long, unhurried meal.

**Order in two rounds.** Start with something from the tandoor, then decide on mains once you have settled in. The kitchen cooks to order, so there is no need to order everything at once.

## A birthday dinner for two

Birthday celebrations for couples are one of the most common reasons people book an evening table with us. Tell us it is a birthday when you message. If you want to bring a cake, ask first so we can tell you what works on that evening. We don't sell decoration packages, and we won't promise a set-up we haven't confirmed for your date.

## Getting here

You will find us on outer Pakhowal Road at VPO Daad, across from Cerajot Tiles. The drive is about 6.8 km from Sarabha Nagar and about 6.5 km from Dugri. Leave extra time on weekend evenings, when Pakhowal Road is busier. The [visit page](../../visit/) has directions and a map. Note that the venue has no wheelchair access.

## Booking

Message or call 096467 13555 with the date, your arrival time and that the table is for two. We confirm what is available that evening. We are open every day from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
