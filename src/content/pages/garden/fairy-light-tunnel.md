The fairy-light tunnel at Red Wood Farm is a walk-through tunnel of warm white fairy lights over a red carpet, with green uplights shining up into the trees around it. It's at our garden restaurant on outer Pakhowal Road, near Daad village. Come after dark to see it properly. We're open every day until 11 PM.

## What does the fairy-light tunnel look like?

Picture an arched walkway hung with strings of small warm white bulbs. In our photos the strings run overhead and hang down both sides, so you're inside a soft glow once you step in. Underfoot is a red carpet that runs the length of the tunnel. Around it, green uplights light the trees from below, which adds a cool green edge to the warm white.

It's the spot guests photograph more than any other part of the garden. What you can count on is what's in our own photos: warm white lights, the red carpet and the green-lit trees.

## When is the best time to see the tunnel?

After dark. Small bulbs barely register against daylight. Once the sky goes dark, the warm white lights take over and the green uplights in the trees show up.

Sunset in Ludhiana comes earlier in winter and later in summer, so the time to arrive shifts through the year. A good plan is to book a dinner table for shortly after sunset. You get a few minutes of deep blue sky, which photographs well, and then full darkness while you eat.

If other guests are taking photos when you arrive, wait for a gap and give each group a turn. A clear shot of the empty carpet is worth a short wait.

## How do you take good photos in a fairy-light tunnel?

These are general tips for any phone in low light. They work well in a tunnel like ours.

**Set up the phone**

- Wipe the lens first. Fingerprints turn every bulb into a smear.
- Turn the flash off. Flash lights the person's face flat and drowns out the glow of the bulbs.
- Use night mode if your phone has it, and hold still for the full count.
- Tap on the person's face to focus, then drag the exposure down a little so the bulbs don't turn into white blobs.

**Frame the shot**

- Shoot down the length of the tunnel. The lines of lights and the red carpet lead the eye toward the person.
- Crouch low to get more of the red carpet in the frame.
- Have your subject stand a few steps inside, with the tunnel stretching out behind them. Distant bulbs blur into soft circles, especially in portrait mode.
- Leave space above the head so the arch of lights shows.

**Think about color**

- The light in the tunnel is warm white, so skin looks warm. If photos look too orange, adjust the warmth slider afterward.
- The carpet is red. Clothes in white, cream, gold or dark green stand out against it. A red outfit tends to blend into the carpet.

**For video**

- Walk slowly toward the camera, or have the person filming walk slowly backward down the carpet.
- Keep the phone at chest height and move smoothly. Short clips of 5 to 10 seconds are easier to keep steady.

## What else lights up in the garden at night?

Other parts of the grounds light up too. Palms along the driveway are wrapped in fairy lights, and so are several trees. Garden paths have lamp posts, and the lawn has low lounge seating under string lights. There's also a green neon "RED WOOD FARM" sign that makes a simple backdrop for a group photo. ## What should you know before you go?

- **Hours:** 10 AM to 11 PM, every day.
- **Access:** there's no wheelchair access.
- **Food:** plan your photos around a meal. Our [menu](../../menu/) lists every dish with prices.
- **Getting here:** from Sarabha Nagar it's about 6.8 km and 11 minutes, from Dugri about 6.5 km and 12 minutes, and from Ludhiana Railway Station about 10.7 km and 16 minutes. Those times assume no traffic, so leave extra time in the evening.
