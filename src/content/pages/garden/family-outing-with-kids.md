For a family outing near Ludhiana, plan an afternoon or evening at Red Wood Farm, a garden restaurant at Daad on outer Pakhowal Road. It is about 6.8 km from Sarabha Nagar. Children have open grass to roam, adults get a full North Indian menu, and we are open from 10 AM to 11 PM every day.

## How far is it from your part of Ludhiana?

Red Wood Farm sits where the city starts to thin out, on the stretch of Pakhowal Road that OpenStreetMap labels Jodhan - Ludhiana Road. Most of the drive is on Pakhowal Road itself. These road distances and times come from OpenStreetMap and OSRM routing, checked on 17 November 2026. The times assume empty roads, so add a margin for traffic.

## An afternoon plan that works with small children

1. **Arrive around lunchtime.** In daylight the lawn is set with long wooden tables and lounge seating, so a family can spread out.
2. **Order early.** Children get hungry faster than adults admit. Put in their food first, then take your time with the rest.
3. **Let them use the grass.** While the curries cook, the kids can stretch their legs on the lawn within sight of your table.
4. **Take a slow walk.** The stone garden path winds through the grounds, and the hedge entrance arch with its flowers makes an easy family photo.
5. **Leave before naps go wrong.** An early afternoon visit gets everyone home before the evening.

## An evening plan for older children

Older kids tend to like the lights. Arrive a little before sunset so they can see the lawn by day first. After dark the fairy-light tunnel is lit, with warm white bulbs over a red carpet and green uplights in the trees, and most visitors stop there for a photo. Have dinner, then head home before our 11 PM closing.

## What is there for children, and what isn't?

There is space on the grass. That is the honest list. Red Wood Farm has no play area, no kids' zone and no rides, so plan on the lawn and the food doing the work. Keep an eye on small children near the paths and the lit decorations, and bring anything that usually keeps them busy at a restaurant table. A spare set of clothes helps too, since grass and small children tend to meet. If a child has a food allergy, message us before the visit and ask about the dishes you plan to order.

## Food that keeps a mixed table happy

Children often lean toward the snack section. French Fries are ₹195, Cheesy Fries ₹225 and Peri Peri Fries ₹225. Honey Chilli Potato and Hakka Noodles are ₹255 each, Cheese Fingers ₹315 and Chicken Popcorn ₹385. A Cheese Naan at ₹80 is a safe bet for picky eaters.

For the adults, Dal Makhni at ₹275 and a half Kadhai Paneer at ₹225 make a solid vegetarian base. Butter Chicken is ₹415 for a half and ₹695 for a full. Finish with ice cream by the scoop: Vanilla and Strawberry are ₹50, Chocolate and Tutti Frutti are ₹55. See more ideas in our guide to [kid-friendly dishes](../../food/kids-favourites/).

## Bringing grandparents along

Red Wood Farm has no wheelchair access, and we would rather you know that before you set out. The seating is outdoors on a lawn, and you reach it on garden paths. If an older family member finds walking hard, message us and ask which table is easiest to reach.

## Other good places to visit in Ludhiana with family

Ludhiana has well-known city parks, such as Nehru Rose Garden and Rakh Bagh, and some families pair a park visit with a meal later in the day. Check their timings before you go, since we do not keep track of them.

Closer to us, OpenStreetMap shows Gurdwara Bhai Bala Sahib Ji about 1.6 km away and Gurudwara Harkiratgarh Tibbi Sahib about 2.3 km away. Our page on [weekend plans near Ludhiana](../weekend-plans-near-ludhiana/) lays out a few half-day plans around Daad.
