If you are searching for a farm to table restaurant in Ludhiana, here is the honest picture. Red Wood Farm is a garden restaurant on outer Pakhowal Road, and Farm to Table Special Chicken is the name of our house dish (₹415 half, ₹695 full). We do not claim to grow our own produce or source from named farms.

## What does "farm to table" usually mean?

The phrase describes a way of running a kitchen. In its strict sense, a farm to table restaurant buys its main ingredients straight from nearby growers, with few or no middlemen in between. The menu shifts with the harvest. Staff can tell you which farm the vegetables, milk or meat came from, and some restaurants grow part of their produce on their own land.

In everyday use the term is much looser. Around the world, plenty of places use it for a rustic look, a garden setting or a menu with fresh salads. On a sign or a menu, the words alone tell you very little. The useful question is always what a particular restaurant can show you.

## Why the name appears on our menu

Two items on the Red Wood Farm menu carry the label:

- **Farm to Table Special Chicken,** our house signature chicken, ₹415 for a half and ₹695 for a full.
- **Farm to Table Special Herbs Chutney,** our own herb chutney, made in the house, ₹75.

These are dish names. They point to the house recipe and the garden we serve it in. They are not a sourcing promise, and we would rather say that plainly than let the name do work it has not earned.

The same goes for the word in our own name. Red Wood Farm is a garden family restaurant and party lawn at VPO Daad, near Daad village. You eat on a lawn, walk a stone path through the grounds and sit under palms wrapped in lights. One guest, gurvinder kaur, wrote in a Google review that it "felt like close to nature". That is the honest appeal of the place.

## What you can expect at Red Wood Farm

- **A garden setting.** An open grass lawn with dining tables, trees, a stone path and, in the evening, fairy lights and string lights.
- **Our own house dishes.** Farm to Table Special Chicken, the herb chutney, and Atta Chicken at ₹925, which is chicken cooked sealed inside wheat-flour dough.
- **A clear menu.** 187 dishes with prices that include GST, and every dish marked veg or non-veg.
- **Honest seasonal marks.** Saag, Bhindi, Gajrela, Malpua Rabri and the whole fish section are marked seasonal, so they are not available all year. Ask us before you come for one of them.

## What you should not expect

- **A list of named farms or suppliers.** We have not published one.
- **Vegetables grown on the premises.** We do not say that we grow produce here.
- **Organic claims.** We make none.
- **A working farm visit.** The grounds are a garden restaurant and party lawn.

A few menu names sound farm-fresh too, such as the Garden Fresh Kachumber at ₹125. Read those as the name of a salad, in the same spirit as the chicken.

## How to ask about ingredients

If where your food comes from matters to you, because of an allergy, a diet or a guest with strict rules, message us at 096467 13555 before your visit. Ask about the exact dish you have in mind, and we will tell you what we can confirm.

One example of how we handle unknowns: the Ice Cream with Brownie is marked non-veg on our menu until the egg question is confirmed. When we are unsure, the menu marks it the cautious way. You can read more about how the marks work on our page about [veg and non-veg marks and GST](../../food/menu-marks-and-gst/).
