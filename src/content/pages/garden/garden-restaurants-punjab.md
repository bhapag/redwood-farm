A garden restaurant in Punjab is somewhere you eat outdoors, on a lawn or among trees, usually from a North Indian and tandoor menu, and often with space for family functions. To choose one, check the menu prices, the evening lighting, the drive time, accessibility and how booking works. Red Wood Farm on Pakhowal Road, Ludhiana is one honest example.

## What makes a restaurant a garden restaurant?

The difference is where you sit. At a regular restaurant you're inside four walls. At a garden restaurant most of the tables are outside, on grass or under trees, with plants and open sky around you.

Across Punjab, these places often sit on the edge of a city, where there's land for a lawn. That means a short drive out of town for most people. In return you get space between tables, fresh air and space for children to walk around on the grass.

Most garden restaurants in the state serve North Indian food, with a tandoor at the center of the menu. Many also take bookings for small functions on the lawn, such as birthdays, kitty parties and family get-togethers.

## What should you check before choosing one?

Use this list for any garden restaurant you're considering. It works anywhere in Punjab.

1. **A menu with prices you can see.** Ask for the menu, or look for one online. You should know what a curry and a naan cost before you sit down.
2. **Veg and non-veg marks.** If your group mixes vegetarians and meat eaters, clear marks on the menu make ordering easier.
3. **Whether prices include GST.** Some menus include it and some add it at the bill. Ask.
4. **Opening hours.** Check the closing time if you want to stay after dark.
5. **Evening lighting.** A lawn looks different at night. Ask for night photos, not only daytime ones.
6. **Cover for bad weather.** Ask what happens if it rains or turns cold.
7. **Accessibility.** If someone in your group uses a wheelchair, ask directly about access before you travel.
8. **How booking works.** Find out if you book by phone, message or a website, and what details they need.
9. **Recent reviews with dates.** A rating means more when you know when it was checked.

## How do Punjab's seasons change the experience?

Punjab has hot summers and cold winters, and a garden restaurant feels the change more than an indoor one.

- **Summer.** Afternoons can be very hot. Most people pick an evening table once the sun is down.
- **Monsoon.** Rain can interrupt outdoor plans, so ask about covered seating and keep a backup time.
- **Winter.** Evenings get cold and foggy. Dress warmly. Daytime lunches in the winter sun are pleasant on a lawn.
- **Spring and autumn.** These are the easy months for eating outside at almost any hour.

Seasonal dishes follow the weather too. On our own menu, Saag, Gajrela and the fish dishes are marked seasonal.
