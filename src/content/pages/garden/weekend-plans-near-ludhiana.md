An easy weekend plan on the outskirts of Ludhiana starts with a drive out along Pakhowal Road to Daad, about 6.5 to 7 km from Dugri, Sarabha Nagar or Model Town. Visit a nearby gurdwara, then spend the afternoon or evening at Red Wood Farm, our garden restaurant, open from 10 AM to 11 PM daily.

## Why outer Pakhowal Road suits a short weekend

A weekend does not have to mean a long highway drive. Outer Pakhowal Road is close enough that you spend minutes in the car instead of hours, yet the built-up city gives way to villages and open ground along the way. You can leave after a late breakfast and be back home for the evening, or go out at sunset and be home by bedtime.

The drive is simple from several city neighborhoods. According to OpenStreetMap and OSRM routing, checked on 17 November 2026:

- **From Model Town** (6.9 km, about 11 minutes with no traffic): Model Town Road, Krishna Mandir Road, then Pakhowal Road.
- **From Dugri** (6.5 km, about 12 minutes): Phase 1 Road and Jawadi Road before you join the main road.
- **From Sarabha Nagar** (6.8 km, about 11 minutes): a short hop onto Pakhowal Road.

Every route ends on the same stretch, which OpenStreetMap names Jodhan - Ludhiana Road. Those times assume empty roads, so leave extra time. Our [Pakhowal Road guide](../../visit/pakhowal-road-guide/) explains how the road runs out of the city.

## A Saturday plan: gurdwara in the morning, lunch on the lawn

1. **Morning.** Visit Gurdwara Bhai Bala Sahib Ji, about 1.6 km from us on the map. Carry a scarf or handkerchief to cover your head, as at any gurdwara.
2. **Late morning.** Drive the short distance to Red Wood Farm. We open at 10 AM.
3. **Lunch.** Sit at the long wooden tables on the daytime lawn. Start with a Tandoori Veg Platter at ₹525 or a Non-Veg Platter at ₹775, then share a Dal Makhni at ₹275 and a half Paneer Lababdar at ₹255 with a basket of breads.
4. **Afternoon.** Walk the stone garden path through the grounds before heading home.

## A Sunday plan: late afternoon into the evening

1. **Around 4 or 5 PM.** Arrive while there is still daylight on the lawn and order something light, such as Honey Chilli Potato at ₹255 or a Masala Papad at ₹75.
2. **Dusk.** Watch the palms on the driveway and the trees light up. The fairy-light tunnel, warm white lights over a red carpet, is where most people take photos.
3. **Dinner.** Move to the tandoor and the curries. Our house signature, Farm to Table Special Chicken, is ₹415 for a half and ₹695 for a full.
4. **Home.** We close at 11 PM, which leaves time for an unhurried meal before an early Monday.

## What about a weekend getaway near Ludhiana?

Searches for weekend getaways near Ludhiana usually turn up hill towns that take most of a day to reach. This page covers the other kind of weekend: a few hours out of the city and home the same night. Red Wood Farm serves meals, so plan a day or evening visit and drive back after dinner.
