For a winter dinner outdoors in Ludhiana, Red Wood Farm serves on its garden lawn on outer Pakhowal Road every evening until 11 PM. In December and January, dress warm, start with a hot soup priced ₹145 to ₹265 and order from the tandoor. Message us first to ask what is set up on the lawn that night.

## How cold does a winter night on the lawn feel?

December and January are the coldest part of the year in Ludhiana. Once the sun drops, an open lawn loses its warmth fast, and on some nights fog rolls in across the region. We are at Daad, on the edge of the city, with open sky above the tables. That is the charm of eating here, and it is also why a light sweater is not enough.

There is a good side to the early sunset. By the time most families sit down for dinner, the garden is already lit: palms along the driveway wrapped in fairy lights, lamp posts along the paths, trees strung with warm bulbs. In winter you get the whole lit garden at 7 PM instead of waiting for a late summer dusk.

## What should you wear for dinner outside?

- **A real jacket or a heavy shawl,** with a sweater underneath. Layers you can take off are easier than one thin coat.
- **Closed shoes and socks.** Grass can turn damp after dark in the cold months.
- **A cap or muffler** for children and grandparents, who tend to feel the cold first.
- **A blanket in the car** if small children are likely to fall asleep on the drive home.

If someone in your group feels the cold badly, plan a shorter sitting. Order the starters and mains together so the food arrives close to the time you sit down.

## What warming is set up at the venue?

We would rather tell you on the day than promise it on a web page. What we can set up for warmth changes from night to night, so send a message to 096467 13555 before you leave home and ask what is on the lawn that evening. If you are bringing older relatives, ask about the most sheltered table as well.

For some events we have put up a covered tent dining area with chandeliers. Whether anything covered is in place on an ordinary dinner night is another good question for the same message.

## Tandoor dishes for a cold evening

Food from the clay oven reaches the table hot, which counts for a lot when the air is cold. Chicken eaters can pick Tandoori Chicken at ₹375, Bhatti Da Murg at ₹395, Pindi Seekh Kabab at ₹395 or Punjabi Murg Tikka at ₹415. On the vegetarian side, try Paneer Tikka at ₹375, Mushroom Tikka at ₹395, Hariyali Tikka at ₹325 or Aloo Nazakat at ₹245.

For a group, a platter saves passing plates around in the cold: the Tandoori Veg Platter is ₹525 and the Tandoori Non-Veg platter is ₹725. Sizzlers arrive on a hot plate, which suits a winter table well. The Veg Sizzler is ₹395 and the Non-Veg Sizzler is ₹475.

Keep the breads coming. Butter Naan is ₹45, Garlic Naan ₹55, Laccha Paratha ₹45 and Missi Roti ₹30. Our [tandoor guide](../../food/tandoor-guide/) goes through the whole section dish by dish.

## Seasonal winter dishes on our menu

Three dishes on our menu carry a seasonal mark, and all three belong to a Punjabi winter:

- **Saag (seasonal), ₹275.** Pair it with Missi Roti at ₹30 or Butter Roti at ₹25.
- **Gajrela, 100 g (seasonal), ₹75.** The slow-cooked carrot sweet that shows up on Punjabi tables once the cold sets in.
- **Malpua Rabri (seasonal), ₹65.** Fried malpua served with rabri.

Seasonal means these dishes are not on every day of the year. If one of them is the reason for your visit, ask us before you drive out. For more on what Punjab eats in the cold months, read our guide to [Punjabi winter food](../../guides/punjabi-winter-food/).

## Winter dates worth planning ahead

The cold months bring Christmas, New Year's Eve and Lohri in mid-January. These are the dates people most often plan family dinners and small get-togethers around. If you want a table, or space for a group, on one of them, send your date, time and number of guests early. Our [Lohri celebration page](../../party/lohri-celebration-ludhiana/) covers small Lohri gatherings on the lawn.

## Before you set out

- We are open 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM every day. An early dinner around 7 PM leaves time to sit without rushing.
- From Sarabha Nagar the drive is about 6.8 km, roughly 11 minutes by OSRM routing with no traffic. In fog, allow a lot longer. Full routes are on our [visit page](../../visit/).
- There is no wheelchair access at Red Wood Farm.
- On a night that feels too cold to sit out, order soups and tandoor dishes for takeaway by phone or message.
- To book a table, send the date, time and number of guests to 096467 13555.
