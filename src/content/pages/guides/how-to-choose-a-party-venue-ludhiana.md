To choose a party venue in Ludhiana, fix your guest count and budget first, then compare venues on five points: indoor or outdoor space, whether food is cooked in-house, the time slot, travel time for your guests, and access for older relatives. Get every answer in writing before you pay an advance.

The rest of this guide takes those points one at a time. We run a garden restaurant and party lawn on Pakhowal Road, so we use Red Wood Farm as a worked example throughout, including the places where we're the wrong fit.

## Start with two numbers: guests and budget

Write down the guest list before you call anyone. Then estimate how many will really come. Some people drop out, and some bring an extra family member or two. A range such as "40 to 50" is more useful to a venue than a single hopeful number.

Next, set a budget per head. Food is usually the largest cost at an Indian party, so most venues price around the menu and the headcount. Decoration, cake and return gifts sit on top of that.

With those two numbers, you can rule out half the venues on your list in one evening of WhatsApp messages.

**At Red Wood Farm:** we don't publish a capacity figure. Tell us your headcount and we'll say honestly whether the lawn works for it. Our restaurant menu is public, with every price including GST, so you can see what the food costs before you ask.

## Indoor or outdoor

This choice shapes everything else, from the decoration to the weather plan. We've written a full comparison in [outdoor vs indoor party venues](../outdoor-vs-indoor-party-venues/), so here is only the short version.

An outdoor lawn gives space, open sky and lights that do part of the decorating after dark. An indoor hall gives you control over heat, cold and rain. If you pick outdoors, ask every venue where your party will go if the weather turns on the day.

**At Red Wood Farm:** parties happen on an open grass lawn with string lights, palms wrapped in fairy lights and a walk-through light tunnel. A covered tent dining area with chandeliers has been set up at an event. Ask us on WhatsApp what cover is possible for your date before you commit.

## Food: the venue's kitchen or an outside caterer

Some venues cook everything themselves. Others rent the space and let you bring a caterer. Each has trade-offs.

An in-house kitchen means one bill, one point of contact and a menu you can taste beforehand by eating there. An outside caterer gives you more choice of dishes, but you then coordinate two businesses, and some venues add a charge for it.

Whichever you choose, ask for the menu in writing with prices, and check that veg and non-veg dishes are clearly separated. For quantities, such as how much dal or how many breads per guest, use our [party planning checklist](../../party/party-planning-checklist/).

**At Red Wood Farm:** food comes from our own kitchen, which serves North Indian, tandoor and Indo-Chinese dishes. The [menu](../../menu/) lists 187 dishes, each marked veg or non-veg, and many mains come in half and full portions. If you want to bring an outside caterer, ask us on WhatsApp first.

## Timing: lunch, evening and the length of the slot

Lunch suits small children and elderly guests. Evenings suit adult birthdays, anniversaries and anything that benefits from lights. Weekends and festival weeks book up first, so have a second date in mind.

Ask what time your slot starts and ends, if you can arrive early to set up, and what happens if the party runs past the end time.

**At Red Wood Farm:** we're open every day from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM, so both lunch and evening parties are possible. Confirm your exact slot and set-up time when you book.

## Location and travel for your guests

Look at where most of your guests live, not just where you live. A venue that's ten minutes from your house can be forty minutes for your relatives across town. Share the location pin in the family group before you book and see who objects.

**At Red Wood Farm:** we're on outer Pakhowal Road at VPO Daad, opposite Cerajot Tiles and near Omaxe Flats. By road that's about 6.8 km from Sarabha Nagar, 10.7 km from Ludhiana Railway Station and 8.2 km from the ISBT bus stand. Those figures don't include traffic, so guests should leave extra time in the evening. Route details are on the [Visit page](../../visit/).

## Accessibility

Ask about steps, uneven ground and the walk from the gate to the seating. If an older relative or a guest who uses a wheelchair is coming, this matters more than the decoration.

**At Red Wood Farm:** we have no wheelchair access. If someone in your group needs it, please choose a venue that has step-free access throughout. We'd rather say so now than have a guest stuck at the gate.

## Questions to ask every venue

Send these in one message so the answers come back in writing:

1. Is the space private for our group, or shared with other diners?
2. What exactly is included in the price, and is GST included?
3. Which time slot do we get, and when can we set up?
4. What is the plan if it rains or gets cold?
5. Can we bring our own cake, decoration and music?
6. How much advance do you need, and what happens if we change the date?
7. Can we see the actual area we'll use before booking?
8. Who is our single contact on the day?

## Red flags to watch for

- **No written menu or prices.** If a venue won't put numbers on WhatsApp, the bill can grow later.
- **Photos that aren't of the venue.** Ask for pictures of the exact area you'll use. Our [party page](../../party/) shows only photos taken at Red Wood Farm.
- **A vague weather answer** for an outdoor space.
- **Pressure to pay a large advance today**, with no written terms.
- **Capacity claims that sound padded.** Ask how the space is laid out for your number.
- **No visit allowed** before you book.
