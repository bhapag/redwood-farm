For an anniversary dinner in Ludhiana with an outdoor feel, book an evening table on our garden lawn on Pakhowal Road. You sit under palms wrapped in fairy lights, order from a full North Indian menu, and walk through the fairy-light tunnel afterwards. We can't promise private dining or a candle set-up, so here is what we do offer.

## What we can promise, and what we can't

Red Wood Farm is a family restaurant first and a party lawn second. On most evenings other families are dining nearby, and some nights a party is booked on the lawn. We don't have a private dining area, and we don't arrange candle light dinners.

What you do get is the garden itself:

- round tables under palm trees
- low lounge seating under string lights
- palms along the driveway wrapped in fairy lights
- the walk-through fairy-light tunnel over a red carpet
- the green neon RED WOOD FARM sign

If a quiet evening matters to you, ask us whether a party is booked on your date. Then pick another night if one is.

## Candle light dinner for couples in Ludhiana: our honest answer

Plenty of couples search for a candle light dinner. We don't sell one. Our light comes from fairy lights in the trees, string lights over the lounge seating and lamp posts along the garden paths. It's warm and soft after dark. If you want to bring a small candle for your own table, ask us first.

## Choosing your table

**Under the palms.** A round table beside an uplit palm is the most photographed spot for couples. It feels set apart from the rest of the lawn without being private.

**Low lounge seating.** These seats sit lower under string lights and suit a long, slow meal with plenty of talking.

**When to arrive.** Come just after sunset so you see the lights come up. In the cooler months that is early evening, which also leaves time for photos in the tunnel before you order.

**What to wear.** Ludhiana evenings from November to February get cold on an open lawn. Bring a shawl or a jacket, even if the afternoon was warm.

## What to order for two

All prices are from our [menu](../../menu/) and include GST. Many main dishes come in Half and Full portions, and a half portion with breads usually suits two people.

**To start, pick one:**

- Paneer Tikka (₹375) or Tandoori Chicken (₹375)
- Veg Sizzler (₹395) or Non-Veg Sizzler (₹475), which arrive on a hot plate and feel like an occasion

**Mains:**

- Butter Chicken (₹415 half and ₹695 full)
- Paneer Lababdar (₹255 half and ₹395 full) for a vegetarian couple
- Dal Makhni (₹275) next to either

**Breads:** Garlic Naan (₹55) and Laccha Paratha (₹45).

**Dessert:** Paneer Jalebi with Rabri (₹75), one each, or Rasmalai (₹45). In season, Malpua Rabri (₹65) is worth asking about.

If you want something different from your usual order, try our house signature dish, Farm to Table Special Chicken (₹415 half and ₹695 full).

## Family anniversaries on the lawn

Many anniversary dinners aren't for two. A parents' 25th or grandparents' 50th brings the whole family, and the lawn has the space a restaurant table doesn't. Real photos from Red Wood Farm show function set-ups with drapes, buffet counters on the lawn and decorated entry gates. What's possible for your evening depends on the date and your numbers, so send both.

One guest summed up the lawn as a "Great place for family party and social events" (Kala Chahal, Google review).

For a bigger family table, these dishes share well:

- Non-Veg Platter (₹775) or Tandoori Veg Platter (₹525)
- Atta Chicken (₹925), chicken cooked sealed inside wheat-flour dough
- Shahi Paneer (₹225 half and ₹375 full) with Butter Naan (₹45)
- Gulab Jamun (₹35) to finish

For a roka, an engagement or a bigger get-together, our page on [small family functions](../small-family-functions/) covers planning in more detail. For more about eating outdoors here, read our [open air dining guide](../../garden/open-air-dining-ludhiana/).

## Getting here for dinner

We're at 676, VPO Daad, Pakhowal Road, opposite Cerajot Tiles and near Omaxe Flats. From Sarabha Nagar it's about 6.8 km and from Dugri about 6.5 km, roughly 11 to 12 minutes by route data that ignores traffic. Leave extra time on a weekend evening. There is no wheelchair access. Full directions are on our [Visit page](../../visit/).
