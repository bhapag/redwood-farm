A good baby shower venue in Ludhiana keeps the mother-to-be comfortable: a daytime slot, a proper chair with back support, a calm spot and food that isn't heavy. Red Wood Farm on Pakhowal Road has a garden lawn with long wooden tables by day and a full menu from 10:00 AM. Plan the day around her.

## Put the mother-to-be's comfort first

Every other choice follows from this one.

- **Daytime works best.** A late-morning or early-afternoon start means she can rest before and get home well before evening.
- **A real chair.** Our lawn has dining tables and low lounge seating. Ask for a regular dining chair for her, because low seats are hard to rise from late in pregnancy.
- **Keep her in one place.** Seat her near the table where gifts are opened and the cake is cut, so guests come to her.
- **Mind the season.** In the hot months, a late-morning start is kinder than mid-afternoon. In winter, the midday sun on the lawn is pleasant.
- **Plan the walk.** Guests reach the lawn along garden paths and grass, and there is no wheelchair access.
- **Two to three hours is enough.** Long enough for food, a few games and photos.
- **Let her choose.** The guest list, the games and the menu should suit her, not the loudest aunt.

## What is a normal budget for a baby shower?

There is no single normal figure. The total depends mostly on how many people come, because food for each guest is usually the biggest cost. Decoration, the cake and small return gifts come next.

Start by fixing the guest count, then set a ceiling for each of those four items. A small lunch and a large party are different budgets, even at the same venue.

We don't publish package or per-plate prices. As a reference, our [menu](../../menu/) lists every dish with its exact price, GST included. Send your date and guest count on WhatsApp and we'll talk through options.

## In which month is a baby shower usually held?

Many families hold it in the third trimester, often around the seventh month, when the pregnancy shows but going out is still manageable. Customs differ from family to family and between communities, so follow what your own family prefers. If the mother-to-be has been told to rest, check with her doctor before fixing the date.

## Who usually pays for a baby shower?

There is no fixed rule. Sometimes close friends or a sister host it. Sometimes it's one set of grandparents-to-be, and sometimes the couple hosts it themselves. Agree on it early in the family group chat, so the bill doesn't become a surprise on the day.

## Games and small touches

Keep games gentle and seated. A few that work at any baby shower:

- **Wishes jar.** Guests write a wish for the baby on a slip of paper. The parents read them later.
- **Bump guess.** Each guest cuts a length of ribbon to the size they think fits around the bump. The closest one wins.
- **Parents' baby photos.** Show one baby photo of each parent and let guests guess whose features the baby will get.
- **Advice cards.** Every guest writes one practical tip for the first month.
