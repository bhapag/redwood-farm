The right birthday party place in Ludhiana fits your guest count, your time of day and your food budget per head. Shortlist two or three venues, then compare the space, the menu prices and the drive for most guests. Red Wood Farm, a garden lawn and family restaurant on outer Pakhowal Road, hosts birthdays from lunch to late evening.

## How do you choose a birthday venue in Ludhiana?

Search results for this lean on directory sites that list banquet halls, cafes and lawns side by side. They are useful for names. They rarely tell you what the evening will feel like. Cut the list down with a few plain checks:

- **Count heads first.** A party for 20 children usually brings parents, siblings and a grandparent or two. Add a few seats for them.
- **Choose lunch or evening.** Young children tend to cope better at lunch, before they get tired. Adult birthdays suit the evening, when lights do much of the decorating on a lawn.
- **Ask where you will actually sit.** A private corner, a shared dining room and an open lawn are very different for a group.
- **Check the drive.** If most guests live in Dugri or Sarabha Nagar, a venue on their side of the city saves arguments.
- **Get the menu in writing.** Prices, veg and non-veg marks, and whether GST is included.

## Birthday party places with price: what to ask

Most venues in the city quote a price per guest, often bundled with decoration, that moves with the date and the guest count. Before you compare quotes, ask each venue what is included: starters, dessert, decoration, the time slot and taxes. Two quotes that look far apart often cover different things.

At Red Wood Farm the party price depends on the menu you pick and how many guests come, so we quote it for your date.
