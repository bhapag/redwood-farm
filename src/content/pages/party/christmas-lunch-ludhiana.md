For a Christmas lunch or family party in Ludhiana, Red Wood Farm on outer Pakhowal Road gives you two choices: a midday table on the garden lawn, or dinner after dark when the palms and tunnel glow with fairy lights. We open 10 AM to 11 PM every day and serve our regular menu. Book on WhatsApp at 096467 13555.

## Lunch in the winter sun or dinner under the lights?

December in Ludhiana shapes when you eat outdoors. Afternoons can be pleasant in the sun, though fog is common in Punjab in late December, and evenings turn cold fast.

### Christmas lunch

A lunch from noon gives you daylight and easier photos of the children. In daytime the lawn has long wooden tables and lounge seating. A family that wants a slow day can stay for two or three hours and finish with dessert as the light fades.

### Christmas dinner

After dark the garden looks most like the season. The walk-through tunnel has warm white fairy lights over a red carpet, the palms along the driveway are wrapped in lights, and the lawn has low seating under string lights. On a December evening, that is the backdrop most families want for a Christmas photo. Bring warm layers, because the lawn is open air.

## What goes on the table without a Christmas menu?

We don't run a special Christmas menu, so plan the meal from the regular one. That has one advantage: you know every price before you arrive, and each one includes GST. A Christmas table from our menu could run like this.

**Soup for a cold evening:** Cream of Tomato (₹155), Sweet Corn Soup (₹155), or Smoked Chicken (₹265) from the soup list.

**The roast-style centerpiece:** a Christmas table usually has a roast, and the nearest thing on our menu is the tandoor. Try Tandoori Chicken (₹375), Tangri Chicken (₹375), or the Tandoori Non-Veg platter (₹725) to share. Atta Chicken (₹925), chicken cooked sealed inside wheat-flour dough, is the highest-priced dish on the menu. Vegetarians can have the Tandoori Veg Platter (₹525) or the Veg Sizzler (₹395).

**Mains:** Butter Chicken (₹415 half, ₹695 full), Paneer Lababdar (₹255 half, ₹395 full) and Dal Makhni (₹275), with Garlic Naan (₹55) and Cheese Pulao (₹280).

**Dessert:** Ice Cream with Brownie (₹95) is the closest thing to a Christmas pudding we have. It is marked non-veg on the menu until we confirm whether the brownie contains egg. Gajrela (100 g, seasonal) at ₹75 is a winter dessert when it is available.

Our [tandoor guide](../../food/tandoor-guide/) has more on the tandoor dishes, and the [full menu](../../menu/) lists all 187 items with prices.

## What do children do on Christmas Day here?

There is space on the grass for children to move around after the meal. There is no play area, no kids' zone and no rides, so plan something to keep them busy and keep an eye on them. Easy orders for kids include French Fries (₹195), Cheesy Fries (₹225), Chicken Popcorn (₹385) and a scoop of Vanilla ice cream (₹50). For more ideas, see [family outings with kids](../../garden/family-outing-with-kids/).

## Can we bring a cake, a Santa hat or gifts?

Families celebrating Christmas often bring a plum cake, a Secret Santa game or wrapped gifts for the children. Tell us on WhatsApp what you are bringing, and we will tell you what is possible at your table. If an older relative is coming, note that Red Wood Farm has no wheelchair access.

## What about a Christmas party with friends or a small team?

Not every Christmas table is family. Friends who swap gifts every year, or a small office team closing out the year, often want a relaxed evening without anyone cooking. For a group like that, order platters first and let people pick. The Chinese Veg Platter (₹575) and the Non-Veg Platter (₹775) give a table plenty to share, with Honey Chilli Potato (₹255) and Chicken Noodles (₹345) for the Indo-Chinese side. Add one dal and one curry once everyone has arrived. A Secret Santa round fits neatly between the starters and the mains.

## How do we reach Red Wood Farm on Christmas Day?

We are at 676, VPO Daad, Pakhowal Road, opposite Cerajot Tiles and near Omaxe Flats. By road it is about 6.5 km from Dugri, 6.9 km from Model Town and 10.7 km from Ludhiana Railway Station, or about 12, 11 and 16 minutes when the roads are clear. Holiday evenings can be slower, so leave a little early. The [visit page](../../visit/) has the map pin to share with relatives coming from other parts of the city.

## Booking Christmas at Red Wood Farm

Message 096467 13555 on WhatsApp, or call, with the date, the time and how many people are coming. Christmas is a public holiday, so message a few days ahead. Read more about [winter evenings on the lawn](../../garden/winter-evenings-on-the-lawn/), or see the [party page](../) for group events.
