A first birthday party goes best as a short daytime gathering of about two hours, timed after your baby's nap, at a place where grandparents can sit comfortably and adults get a proper meal. Red Wood Farm on outer Pakhowal Road has a garden lawn and a North Indian menu from 10:00 AM daily.

## Who the party is really for

Your baby won't remember the day. The family will remember the photos, the blessings from the elders and the meal. Most guests at a first birthday are grown-ups, so plan the food, seating and timing for them. Then give your baby a calm 30 to 45 minutes in the middle for the cake and the pictures.

## How to host a 1st birthday party

1. **Fix the time around naps.** Many one-year-olds still nap once in the morning and once in the afternoon. A party that starts soon after a nap catches your baby fresh. A late evening party often ends with the guest of honour asleep before the cake.
2. **Keep it to about two hours.** That is long enough for a meal and cake, and short enough for a cheerful baby.
3. **Write the guest list by household.** Grandparents, aunts and uncles, and a few friends with babies of their own. Count adults and children separately when you message us.
4. **Choose daytime or evening.** By day the lawn has long wooden tables and lounge seating. After dark the string lights and the fairy-light tunnel take over. The evening looks lovely in photos, though it runs later than most babies like.
5. **Book a table.** Send the date, start time and both head counts to 096467 13555.
6. **Pack a proper baby bag.** A spare outfit (cake happens), a changing mat, wipes, a light blanket, your baby's own food and spoon, and ear muffs if there will be music.

## How to make baby's first birthday special

The moments people talk about later rarely cost much.

- **A handprint card.** Press your baby's hand in paint on a sheet of card, and ask each guest to sign around it.
- **Letters for age 18.** Hand every guest a card and envelope. They write a note for your child to open at eighteen, and you seal the lot in one box.
- **One photo with each grandparent.** Use the same spot every time, such as the flower entrance arch by day or the fairy-light tunnel after sunset. The set of matching pictures becomes a keepsake.
- **A month-by-month photo line.** Peg twelve prints, one from each month, on a string between two chairs.
- **A short cake moment.** Bring a bib and a mat, let your baby touch the cake for a minute, and then take the plate away before it ends up on Grandma.
- **Short video messages.** Ask each elder to record 30 seconds on your phone, speaking to your child.

## Seating and comfort for grandparents

Elders usually stay longest at a first birthday, so look after them first.

- Ask for a table close to where the cake is cut, so they don't need to walk across the lawn.
- Low lounge seating can be hard to get out of. Request regular dining chairs for older guests when you book.
- Red Wood Farm has no wheelchair access. Tell relatives in advance so nobody is caught out on the day.
- On winter evenings, bring shawls. On warm days, a table under the palm trees is a calmer spot than open grass.
- Share the location pin in the family group. We're at 676, VPO Daad, Pakhowal Road, opposite Cerajot Tiles. From Model Town it's about 6.9 km, and from Sarabha Nagar about 6.8 km. Leave extra time for traffic. Directions are on our [Visit page](../../visit/).
