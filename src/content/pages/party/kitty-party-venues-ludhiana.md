A kitty party venue in Ludhiana needs three things: a daytime slot that starts when your group wants it, one table where everyone sits together for a few hours, and a menu with prices you can read. Red Wood Farm opens at 10 AM daily on outer Pakhowal Road, with lawn seating and a full veg and non-veg kitchen.

## How to host a kitty party at a restaurant

Hosting at a venue saves the host a day of cooking and cleaning up. The steps are short:

1. **Fix the date and the host for the month.** Most groups rotate.
2. **Get a firm headcount.** Kitty groups are usually steady, but one or two members bring a guest or drop out.
3. **Book the slot.** Say what time you arrive and roughly how long you plan to stay.
4. **Choose the food.** Either order on the day from the menu, or agree a short list in advance.
5. **Plan the games and the theme.** Our page on [kitty party themes and games](../kitty-party-themes-and-games/) has ideas that work at a table.
6. **Settle the kitty and the bill** before the group starts leaving.

The finer points, such as who invites, how the kitty draw runs and how the host keeps things fair, are in our [kitty party hosting guide](../../guides/kitty-party-hosting-guide/).

## Kitty party halls with price, and how a restaurant compares

Banquet halls in the city usually quote a fixed price per guest with a set menu, and some ask for a minimum number of guests. That suits a large group that wants one bill and no choices. A restaurant lets a smaller group order what it likes and pay for what it eats.

We quote party prices because they depend on your menu and group size. Our [menu](../../menu/) shows every dish price up front, GST included, which lets the host work out a rough spend before booking. When you compare a hall quote with a restaurant, check what the hall price covers: starters, dessert and taxes are the usual gaps.

## Kitty party restaurants: what makes one work

A restaurant table works for a kitty group when a few practical things line up. Run through these before you book anywhere:

- **Room to sit as one group.** Twelve to twenty ladies split across three small tables turns into three separate parties. Ask for long tables or a joined set.
- **Time to linger.** A kitty lunch often runs three hours with the games. Check that nobody will need your table back halfway through.
- **A corner where you can hear each other.** Housie and quiz games need people to hear the caller.
- **A menu that suits mixed diets.** Clear veg and non-veg marks save the host a lot of questions.
- **One person to deal with on the day.** Save that number in the group before the date.

## Getting the group there

Red Wood Farm is at 676, VPO Daad, on the Pakhowal Road side of the city, opposite Cerajot Tiles and close to Omaxe Flats. From Model Town it is about 6.9 km by road, and from Dugri about 6.5 km. Map times come without traffic, so tell the group to leave a little early. Share the pin from our [visit page](../../visit/) in your group chat a day before.

One honest note for planning: there is no wheelchair access. If a member needs it, talk to her before you book.

A Google reviewer, Sanjeev Kumar, wrote that the "staff is also friendly". We'd rather you judge that for yourself over a long lunch.
