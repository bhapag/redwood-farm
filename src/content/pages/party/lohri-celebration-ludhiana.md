## What is Lohri, and why do families gather for it?

Lohri is Punjab's winter festival, held in mid-January, usually on 13 January. It marks the end of the coldest stretch of the year and has long been linked with the farming calendar in Punjab. Traditionally, families and neighbors gather in the evening around a fire, sing folk songs, dance, and share sesame and jaggery sweets, peanuts and popcorn.

A family's first Lohri after a wedding or the birth of a child is often the biggest of all. Relatives come from across the city and from further away, gifts change hands, and the evening runs late. That is usually when people look for a venue, because the guest list outgrows the house.

## What should guests expect on a January evening?

January nights in Ludhiana are cold, and the lawn is open air. Tell guests to bring shawls and warm jackets, especially the elders. Red Wood Farm's own photos show a covered tent dining area with chandeliers set up at one event, so ask whether a covered set-up is possible for your Lohri.

After sunset the garden is lit. A walk-through tunnel of warm white fairy lights runs over a red carpet, the palms along the driveway are wrapped in lights, and lamp posts line the garden paths. Guests photograph the tunnel more than anything else, which makes it a natural spot for the family picture in your Lohri outfits.

## What winter food is on the menu for Lohri?

Lohri food is winter food: greens, butter, jaggery and milk sweets. Our menu has several dishes that suit a January table. Anything marked seasonal depends on availability, so confirm it a few days ahead.

**Seasonal dishes**

- **Saag (seasonal)** at ₹275, a winter staple in Punjab
- **Gajrela (100 g, seasonal)** at ₹75, the carrot and milk pudding many families make every winter
- **Malpua Rabri (seasonal)** at ₹65

The menu has no corn roti. Missi Roti (₹30), Tandoori Roti (₹20) and Butter Naan (₹45) are the breads to pair with Saag.

Sesame sweets, peanuts and popcorn are not on our menu. If you want to bring your own to hand out, ask us first. The [full menu](../../menu/) lists every dish with its price, and our guide to [Punjabi winter food](../../guides/punjabi-winter-food/) covers more of the season's dishes.

## How do you plan a Lohri party for a big family?

- **Fix the date early.** Lohri is one evening, and many families want that same evening.
- **Choose the hour.** It is an evening festival. Starting around sunset gives you the lights, and we close at 11 PM.
- **Count heads twice.** Give a rough number when you book and a firm one closer to the day.
- **Plan for the elders.** There is no wheelchair access at Red Wood Farm, so think ahead for any grandparent who uses a wheelchair.
- **Gifts and decorations.** Tell us if you are bringing a cake, decorations or gifts to hand out.

Our page on [small family functions](../small-family-functions/) covers roka and engagement gatherings, which need much the same planning.

If the party is for a first Lohri, put the couple or the baby's parents at the center table and keep the singing and the gift-giving close to them. Order the starters to arrive as guests settle, so the food is not competing with the singing later in the evening.
