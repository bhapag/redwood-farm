For a New Year's Eve family dinner in Ludhiana, Red Wood Farm on outer Pakhowal Road offers tables on a garden lawn lit with fairy lights and a full menu with GST-inclusive prices. Regular hours run from 10 AM to 11 PM, so treat it as an evening meal and ask us on WhatsApp at 096467 13555 about the night.

## What can we do on New Year's Eve with family?

Plenty of families skip the loud ticketed party and want a good dinner together, a few photos and a safe drive home before the roads fill up. Here is a simple plan that suits small children and grandparents alike.

1. **Eat early.** Book a table for the early evening. The children are still awake and cheerful, and the elders are not out late in the cold.
2. **Walk the tunnel.** After dinner, walk through the fairy-light tunnel, the spot our guests photograph most. Warm white lights over a red carpet make a fine last family photo of the year.
3. **Stop at the name arch.** The "Red Wood Farm" name arch and the green neon sign give you two more backdrops.
4. **See in midnight at home.** Our regular hours end at 11 PM, which leaves time to drive home and watch the countdown together on TV or from the roof.

For the night itself, ask us on WhatsApp what we have planned for New Year's Eve. This page doesn't promise a countdown or late opening.

## What should three generations order?

A table with a seven-year-old and a seventy-year-old needs range. Order a lighter base, a few rich dishes and something the kids will finish.

- **Lighter dishes:** Tomato Shorba (₹145), Yellow Dal Tadka (₹245), Palak Paneer (₹225 half, ₹295 full) and Steamed Rice (₹180).
- **The rich dishes for the night:** Dal Makhni (₹275), Paneer Butter Masala (₹225 half, ₹345 full), Mutton Rogan Josh (₹345 half, ₹645 full) and Farm to Table Special Chicken (₹415 half, ₹695 full), our house signature chicken.
- **For the children:** Cheese Naan (₹80), French Fries (₹195), Veg Cutlet (₹245) and Chicken Popcorn (₹385).
- **To end the year on something sweet:** Paneer Jalebi with Rabri (₹75), Rasmalai (₹45) or Chocolate ice cream (₹55 a scoop).

Half portions on many curries let a family try more without over-ordering. The [full menu](../../menu/) has the rest, and the [group ordering guide](../../food/group-ordering-guide/) explains half and full sizes.

## How do we get there and back on a busy night?

The last night of the year brings extra traffic to the city. The times below come from route data without traffic, so leave well before your booking.

- Pakhowal Road at Hero Bakery Chowk: 6.1 km, about 10 minutes
- Dugri: 6.5 km, about 12 minutes
- Sarabha Nagar: 6.8 km, about 11 minutes
- Model Town: 6.9 km, about 11 minutes

The address for your map is 676, VPO Daad, Pakhowal Road, opposite Cerajot Tiles, near Omaxe Flats, Ludhiana. Directions and the pin are on the [visit page](../../visit/).

## What if we would rather eat at home?

Takeaway works for New Year's Eve too. Order by phone or WhatsApp on 096467 13555, collect the food on your way home and serve it as midnight gets close. A Veg Biryani (₹325) or Non-Veg Biryani (₹375) is an easy one-pot order for a home table, with Boondi Raita (₹165) on the side. Place the order early in the evening, since our regular hours end at 11 PM.

## Could New Year's Day lunch work better?

Some families find the evening of 31 December too crowded, with relatives at other parties and children who won't last until late. A lunch on 1 January is a calmer way to start the year together. We open at 10 AM, so a late-morning table gives you the whole afternoon on the lawn. In daylight the lawn has long wooden tables and lounge seating, and on a clear day the winter sun makes a long lunch easy. Start with Sweet Corn Soup (₹155), move on to Paneer Tikka (₹375) and Tandoori Chicken (₹375), and finish with Kesar Kheer (₹75).

## What should we wear and bring?

The lawn is open air and the last night of December is cold in Punjab. Dress the children in layers and bring a shawl or blanket for the grandparents. If you want to bring a cake to cut for the new year, tell us on WhatsApp and we will tell you what is possible at your table. The same applies to balloons or any small decoration you have in mind.

WhatsApp or call 096467 13555 with the date, your preferred time and the number of guests, and ask what is on for New Year's Eve.
