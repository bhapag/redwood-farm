For a corporate team lunch in Ludhiana, choose a venue that opens before noon, seats the whole team at one table and lists its prices. Red Wood Farm serves from 10 AM every day on outer Pakhowal Road, with Wi-Fi, platters from ₹525, half portions on many curries, and a menu where every price already includes GST.

## What a team lunch costs here

We don't publish a per-person corporate rate. Your bill is simply what the table orders from the [full menu](../../menu/), and every price there is final, GST included. That makes it easy for whoever holds the budget to estimate the spend before booking. If you need a quote for a large team or a fixed menu, ask us.

The sections below pull the dishes that work best for a group, with exact prices.

## Starters and platters for the middle of the table

Platters are the fastest way to feed a team that can't agree. Each one puts several items on one plate:

- **Tandoori Veg Platter** ₹525
- **Chinese Veg Platter** ₹575
- **Tandoori Non-Veg** ₹725
- **Non-Veg Platter** ₹775

If you'd rather order single starters from the tandoor, **Paneer Tikka** and **Tandoori Chicken** are ₹375 each, **Hariyali Tikka** is ₹325 and **Punjabi Murg Tikka** is ₹415. Our [tandoor guide](../../food/tandoor-guide/) covers the whole section. For a quick working lunch, a **Veg Sizzler** (₹395) or **Non-Veg Sizzler** (₹475) gives one person a full plate in one dish.

Every dish on the menu carries a veg or non-veg mark.

## Why a daytime lunch works out here

**Early opening.** The kitchen starts at 10:00 AM, so a late-morning offsite or a lunch that starts at noon is no problem. We stay open until 11:00 PM if the day turns into a team dinner.

**Space to sit together.** In daytime the lawn has long wooden tables and lounge seating, which suit a team that wants one long table for lunch and a softer corner for the conversation after.

**Wi-Fi.** It is available, so a manager can stay on email between courses.

**The drive.** From Sarabha Nagar it is about 6.8 km by Pakhowal Road, around 11 minutes in map routing without traffic. Dugri is about 6.5 km and Model Town about 6.9 km. Lunch hour traffic in the city adds time, so plan a margin. Turn-by-turn notes are on our [route from Sarabha Nagar](../../visit/from-sarabha-nagar/).

There is no wheelchair access, which is worth knowing if anyone on the team needs it.

## A short checklist for whoever organises it

- Confirm the headcount two days ahead and again on the morning of the lunch.
- Note how many are vegetarian and any dish someone avoids.
- Fix a start time and a hard stop if people have afternoon meetings.
- Decide in advance whether the team orders from the menu at the table or you agree a short list of dishes when you book.
- Keep the booking details in one chat so a colleague can take over if you are out.

## Ordering for a group

When you book, send the date, the arrival time, the headcount and how many are vegetarian. That lets us tell you what is possible for the slot. If part of the team can't make the trip, takeaway is also an option: order by phone or message and collect.

Red Wood Farm held a Google rating of 4.2 from 72 reviews on 14 Nov 2026, if your admin team likes to check before booking.
