Red Wood Farm on outer Pakhowal Road works as a retirement or office farewell party venue in Ludhiana. You get a garden lawn with dining tables, string lights after dark and a North Indian and Indo-Chinese menu with GST-inclusive prices. Open 10 AM to 11 PM daily. Send your date and guest count to 096467 13555.

## Should a retirement party be a lunch or a dinner?

It depends on who is retiring and who is coming. A send-off for someone who spent decades in one office usually draws colleagues who are still on duty, so a long lunch on a working day lets them attend without giving up their evening. A school principal or a bank manager with a large family often prefers dinner, when spouses and grown children can come along.

At Red Wood Farm the kitchen starts at 10 AM, so a lunch can begin before noon and still leave room for speeches. By day the lawn has long wooden tables and lounge seating. After dark the palms along the driveway and the trees are wrapped in fairy lights, and the lawn has low seating under string lights. Decide on the mood first, then pick the hour.

## How do you seat colleagues and family together?

Farewells mix two groups who often have never met. Office staff cluster by department, and the family wants to sit close to the guest of honor. Ask us for one long table, or a run of joined tables, with the retiring person in the middle. The family can take one side and colleagues the other, and nobody has to shout across the lawn during the speeches.

For a large group, give us the number when you message. We will reply with what the lawn can take on your date. This page doesn't quote a fixed figure because the layout changes with each event.

## What should a farewell menu include for an office group?

Office groups are always mixed. Some people are vegetarian, some eat chicken but skip mutton, one person avoids spice and the younger staff want Indo-Chinese. Every dish on the [Red Wood Farm menu](../../menu/) carries a veg or non-veg mark, which helps the person ordering for a whole floor of colleagues.

A balanced spread could look like this:

- **Starters to pass around:** Paneer Tikka (₹375), Hariyali Tikka (₹325), Tandoori Chicken (₹375) and Chicken Popcorn (₹385).
- **Mains:** Dal Makhni (₹275), Shahi Paneer (₹225 half, ₹375 full) and Butter Chicken (₹415 half, ₹695 full).
- **One showpiece dish:** Atta Chicken (₹925), chicken cooked sealed inside wheat-flour dough.
- **Breads and rice:** Butter Naan (₹45), Laccha Paratha (₹45) and Jeera Rice (₹230).
- **Dessert:** Rasmalai (₹45) or Kesar Kheer (₹75).

Many main-course dishes come in half portions, so a smaller table can still try several curries. For help working out quantities, the [party planning checklist](../party-planning-checklist/) goes through food per guest.

## Where do the speeches, the cake and the memento fit?

Most retirement parties follow a short program. The boss says a few words, the person retiring replies, someone hands over a shawl or a framed memento, and a cake gets cut. Run the program before the food arrives, while everyone is still seated and listening. Once the main course lands, attention drifts to the plates.

In your message, mention the cake, any banner or memento, and the time the speeches will start. If you want a microphone or music, ask about that too. We will tell you what is possible and where it can go.

## What about older guests and the drive out?

Retirement parties bring out older relatives and former colleagues who retired years ago. Two things matter for them. First, Red Wood Farm has no wheelchair access, so check with any guest who uses a wheelchair before you fix the venue.

Second, the drive. We are at 676, VPO Daad, Pakhowal Road, opposite Cerajot Tiles and near Omaxe Flats. From Sarabha Nagar it is about 6.8 km and from Model Town about 6.9 km, roughly 11 minutes each without traffic. Leave extra time on a weekday evening. The [visit page](../../visit/) has the map pin to forward to your guest list.

## How is an office farewell different from a retirement?

A farewell for a colleague moving to another city or company is usually smaller and younger. It often happens at lunch on the person's last working day, with no formal program at all. The lawn makes an easy break from the office for that kind of goodbye. Order a couple of platters to share, such as the Tandoori Veg Platter (₹525) or the Non-Veg Platter (₹775), add Hakka Noodles (₹255) and French Fries (₹195), and let people come and go.
