Red Wood Farm is a garden restaurant and party lawn on outer Pakhowal Road that suits school batch, college and family reunions in Ludhiana. Your group gets tables on an open lawn, a lit tunnel for the group photo and a menu covering veg, chicken, mutton and Indo-Chinese dishes. Book on WhatsApp at 096467 13555 with your date and numbers.

## Which kinds of reunions does the lawn suit?

### School and college batches

A batch reunion is loud and long. People arrive at different times, hug, change seats and pass phones around to show pictures of their kids. An open lawn handles that kind of movement more easily than a fixed restaurant booth.

### Cousins and the extended family

Family reunions often happen when relatives visit from abroad in winter. Grandparents, parents and children all need something on the menu, and the grass gives kids room to move. There is no play area and there are no rides, so they will need watching.

### Old colleagues

Former office teams who scattered years ago meet more easily on neutral ground. A weekend lunch on the lawn gives them a few hours to catch up without anyone having to host at home.

## How do you get a scattered group to turn up?

Attendance is the hardest part of any reunion. Fix the date at least three weeks out, put one person in charge of the list, and share the exact location before you share anything else. People decide on distance.

Here is how far Red Wood Farm is by road from common starting points. The times assume no traffic, so add a margin.

Classmates coming in by train or bus for the day can take a cab from the station or the ISBT. The [visit page](../../visit/) has the pin and directions for the group chat. The address to paste is 676, VPO Daad, Pakhowal Road, opposite Cerajot Tiles, near Omaxe Flats.

## What should a reunion group order when nobody agrees?

Reunion orders go wrong when one person orders for everyone from memory. Order in rounds instead. Start with platters while people are still arriving, then call for mains once you know who actually came.

Good opening rounds from our [menu](../../menu/):

- Chinese Veg Platter (₹575) and Tandoori Non-Veg (₹725) for the table to share
- Honey Chilli Potato (₹255) and Chilli Paneer (₹375) for the college crowd
- Malai Champ (₹275) or Afghani Champ (₹275), both soya chaap and both vegetarian

For mains, put one dal, one paneer and one chicken or mutton dish on each table. Dal Makhni (₹275), Paneer Butter Masala (₹225 half, ₹345 full) and Mutton Rogan Josh (₹345 half, ₹645 full) cover most tastes. Half portions let a small table try more. Our [group ordering guide](../../food/group-ordering-guide/) explains half and full sizes.

## How long can a reunion run?

We are open from 10 AM to 11 PM every day. A lunch reunion that starts at noon can stretch into the late afternoon, and a dinner can carry on until closing. Tell us your expected start and finish when you book, so the tables are ready and the kitchen can plan for several rounds of orders. If the group grows on the day, message us as soon as you know.

## Where do you take the group photo?

Every reunion ends with someone trying to fit every face into one frame. The fairy-light tunnel at Red Wood Farm is the spot guests photograph most. It is a walk-through tunnel of warm white lights over a red carpet, with green uplights in the trees. A line of old friends walking through it makes a livelier picture than a row of people squinting at a table.

For a daytime shot, the green hedge entrance arch and the "Red Wood Farm" name arch both work. You can read more about the [fairy-light tunnel](../../garden/fairy-light-tunnel/). Take the group photo early, before anyone has to leave.
