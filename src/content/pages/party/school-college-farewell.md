For a school or college farewell party in Ludhiana, Red Wood Farm offers a daytime garden lawn on outer Pakhowal Road, open from 10 AM, with a menu where breads start at ₹20 and every price includes GST. Students order as a group from the regular menu, and teachers or parents can join them. Book at 096467 13555.

## Why hold a farewell in the daytime?

Most school farewells finish before dark because parents want their children home at a sensible hour. College groups often face the same rule from hostels and families. A late-morning or afternoon party settles that. The kitchen at Red Wood Farm opens at 10 AM, and in daylight the lawn has long wooden tables and lounge seating. Red Wood Farm's own photos include a kite-theme backdrop with flowers made for a daytime party, which shows how the lawn looks dressed for a day event.

Daylight also helps the photos. Farewell outfits, sarees and suits get photographed far more than the food. By day, the green hedge entrance arch, the stone garden path and the name arch all make easy backdrops.

## How do students keep the bill inside a budget?

Students usually split the cost, so the plan has to work for the person with the least pocket money. Build the meal around low-priced basics and add a few shared dishes on top.

Low-cost basics on our menu:

- Tandoori Roti (₹20), Butter Naan (₹45) and Aloo Paratha with Butter (₹50)
- Rajmah (₹230), Yellow Dal Tadka (₹245) and Dal Makhni (₹275)
- French Fries (₹195), Honey Chilli Potato (₹255) and Hakka Noodles (₹255)
- Gulab Jamun (₹35), Rasmalai (₹45) and Vanilla ice cream (₹50 a scoop)

Here is one sample order, priced item by item from the menu, to show how the numbers add up. One Dal Makhni (₹275), one full Kadhai Paneer (₹375), one Honey Chilli Potato (₹255), one Hakka Noodles (₹255), one Jeera Rice (₹230), twelve Butter Naan (₹45 each, so ₹540) and twelve Gulab Jamun (₹35 each, so ₹420). The total is ₹2,350, with GST already in it. This is regular menu pricing and not a package. Ask us how far each portion goes for your group before you lock the order.

## How should a big student group order?

A whole class calling out orders at once confuses everyone, the class included. Pick one person to collect choices a day ahead in the class group chat. Ask two questions only: veg or non-veg, and Indian or Indo-Chinese. Every dish on our [menu](../../menu/) carries a veg or non-veg mark, so the organizer can check in seconds. Vegetarian students have plenty to pick from, and our [vegetarian guide](../../food/vegetarian-guide/) lists the paneer, champ and dal dishes.

Order shared dishes for each table instead of individual plates. Farewells in Punjab usually include titles like Mr and Ms Farewell, a few performances and a lot of photos. Let the starters arrive while the titles are being announced, then send for the mains.

## What should parents and teachers know?

Parents usually ask four things: where it is, when it ends, who is in charge and what kind of place it is. Send the answers before they ask.

- **Where:** 676, VPO Daad, Pakhowal Road, opposite Cerajot Tiles, near Omaxe Flats, Ludhiana. From Dugri it is about 6.5 km, and from Model Town about 6.9 km, around 11 to 12 minutes without traffic.
- **When:** a fixed start and finish time. A daytime slot gets everyone home before evening.
- **Who:** one student organizer and one teacher, with their phone numbers.
- **What kind of place:** Red Wood Farm is a family restaurant first, with a garden lawn for parties. Teachers and parents are welcome to come along.

One more point for the organizers: there is no wheelchair access, which matters if a teacher or student uses a wheelchair.

## Can a farewell have decorations, titles and music?

Most farewells have all three. Tell us what you are planning, such as a banner, a balloon arch, a spot for the titles or a speaker for music. Real photos from Red Wood Farm show balloon arches and decorated backdrops at past parties. For your own date, ask us what is possible on the lawn and we will give you a straight answer.

Choose two possible dates, then message 096467 13555 with the date, the start time and the number of students and staff. A teacher or parent can make the booking if that is easier for the school.
