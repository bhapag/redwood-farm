For a small family function in Ludhiana, such as a roka, an engagement or a close-family get-together, Red Wood Farm offers an open garden lawn on outer Pakhowal Road with food from its own North Indian kitchen. Past functions here have used a mehndi stage, draped set-ups and buffet counters on the grass. Send your numbers for a set-up suggestion.

## What past functions on our lawn looked like

Everything in this section comes from Red Wood Farm's own photos of real events.

- **A mehndi stage.** A flower-framed swing under a draped canopy, with flower beds along the front.
- **Draped function set-ups.** Canopies and curtains with green garland trim over the serving area.
- **Buffet counters on the lawn.** A long line of counters along one side, with round tables on the grass in front.
- **A covered tent dining area with chandeliers.** This was seen at one event, so ask whether it suits your date and size.
- **Decorated entry gates.** Guests walk in under a dressed gate before reaching the lawn.

Those set-ups were made for those families. Yours depends on the date, your guest count and what you want, so treat the photos as a starting point for the conversation. One guest wrote that it's "Good space for small function parties.." (Inderpreet Singh, Google review).

## Planning a roka

A roka is usually small and short, with close family from both sides, a simple ceremony and a meal. A daytime slot gives you natural light for family photos on the lawn.

A few things that help:

- Set one table for the elders of both families near the ceremony area, so they can see without standing.
- Mix the seating at lunch so the two families talk to each other.
- Keep the ceremony and the meal in one area of the lawn, which saves older relatives a walk.

## Planning an engagement

An engagement usually has a longer guest list and needs a focal point for the ring exchange: a stage, a decorated corner or a backdrop where the couple stands. An evening engagement gets the string lights and the fairy-light tunnel, which makes a good spot for couple photos between the ceremony and dinner. A daytime engagement gets the long wooden tables and lounge seating on the lawn.

Tell us which you prefer when you message, along with any decoration you plan to bring or arrange, and we'll confirm what works.

## How to plan a small function, step by step

1. **Fix the date and the time of day.** Daytime and evening feel very different on a lawn.
2. **Count guests by group.** Write down adults, elders and children separately, and for a roka or engagement, each family's side too.
3. **Decide how food is served.** Some families order from the menu; larger groups may want buffet counters. Ask us what suits your number.
4. **Say if the function is vegetarian only.** Every dish on our menu carries a veg or non-veg mark, which makes planning a mixed or all-veg spread simple.
5. **List your decoration.** A stage, drapes, an entry gate or flowers. Tell us early so we can confirm what's possible.
6. **Share the location with older relatives.** We're at 676, VPO Daad, Pakhowal Road, opposite Cerajot Tiles. There is no wheelchair access.
7. **Confirm everything in writing.** Keep the final details in one message thread.
