Dugri is about 6.5 km from Red Wood Farm by road, the shortest of the city routes we checked. OSRM takes you along Phase 1 Road, then Jawadi Road, which meets the outer stretch of Pakhowal Road for the final 2.8 km to Daad. The estimate is about 12 minutes with no traffic, so leave a little extra.

## How the Dugri route differs from the others

Most routes from central Ludhiana run up inner Pakhowal Road and follow it out of the city. Dugri's route doesn't. According to OSRM, you cut across on Jawadi Road and join Pakhowal Road only on its outer stretch, the part OpenStreetMap labels "Jodhan - Ludhiana Road". That means you skip the inner city section of Pakhowal Road altogether.

There is one surprise in the numbers. Dugri is the shortest trip by distance, yet OSRM gives it 12 minutes, one minute more than Sarabha Nagar or Model Town. OSRM assigns different speeds to different kinds of road, and it expects a slower average on this route: about 6.5 km in 12 minutes, against 6.8 km in 11 from [Sarabha Nagar](../from-sarabha-nagar/). In real traffic the gap may go either way.

## Roads in order

Checked with OSRM on 17 Nov 2026, using OpenStreetMap road names:

- **Phase 1 Road, about 1.1 km.** You head out of Dugri on this road.
- **Jawadi Road, about 1.7 km.** The connecting leg. It carries you across to Pakhowal Road without entering the inner city stretch. This is the turn to watch. If you miss it, let your navigation reroute you. You may end up on inner Pakhowal Road instead, which also leads out to Daad, only with a little more distance and more junctions.
- **Jodhan - Ludhiana Road, about 2.8 km.** Outer Pakhowal Road, the last leg before our gate.

The three named roads come to about 5.6 km. OSRM's full figure of 6.5 km also includes the streets inside Dugri and short links at junctions that it doesn't name. OSRM started from the point OpenStreetMap treats as the center of Dugri, so your own street may add or remove a few hundred meters.

## Timing your drive

The 12-minute estimate assumes zero traffic. It is a fair guide early in the day and a poor one at peak hours. If you are meeting family for lunch or arriving before guests at a party, allow extra time so you are not the one running late. We are open from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM, every day, so you can plan around the busy hours.

## The last 2.8 km and the gate

When Jawadi Road brings you onto outer Pakhowal Road, you are on the final leg. Keep an eye out for:

1. **Omaxe Flats (Omaxe Royal Residency).** OSRM puts it about 0.8 km from us by road.
2. **Daad village.** It appears as "Dad" on OpenStreetMap and lies under a kilometer from our pin.
3. **Cerajot Tiles.** Red Wood Farm is directly opposite.

Turn in at our garden gate. The green hedge arch with flowers marks the way in, and once inside you will find the "Red Wood Farm" name arch and the paths through the garden.

Please note that Red Wood Farm has no wheelchair access. If anyone in your group uses a wheelchair, plan ahead.

Coming back after dinner, the same roads in reverse take you home: outer Pakhowal Road, Jawadi Road, then Phase 1 Road into Dugri. Your app may choose otherwise at night, and that is fine too.
