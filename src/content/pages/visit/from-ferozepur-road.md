From Bharat Nagar Chowk on Ferozepur Road, Red Wood Farm is about 9.5 km by road. You follow Ferozepur Road for roughly 1.5 km, cross over to Pakhowal Road, and stay on it out of the city to Daad. OSRM puts the drive at about 14 minutes with no traffic. Leave extra time for the real thing.

## Why we measure from Bharat Nagar Chowk

Ferozepur Road is long, so "from Ferozepur Road" could mean almost anything. We picked Bharat Nagar Chowk as a fixed junction that OSRM could route from cleanly. If you start somewhere else on Ferozepur Road, the second half of this route stays the same. Only the first part changes.

## Part one: from Ferozepur Road across to Pakhowal Road

Road names below are OpenStreetMap's, from OSRM's route on 17 Nov 2026.

| Road | About |
|---|---|
| Ferozepur Road Flyover | 0.6 km |
| Ferozepur Road | 0.9 km |
| Pakhowal Road | 1.3 km |
| Satpal Mittal Road | 0.3 km |
| Krishna Mandir Road | 0.5 km |

This is the busy part of the trip. You come off the flyover, run along Ferozepur Road for under a kilometer, then switch to Pakhowal Road. OSRM then steps off Pakhowal Road for two short links, Satpal Mittal Road and Krishna Mandir Road, before putting you back on it. Our route data places Hero Bakery Chowk on Satpal Mittal Road, a useful name if you are giving directions to a driver.

Your own navigation app may take a slightly different line through these junctions depending on traffic. Follow it, as long as it brings you back onto Pakhowal Road heading out of the city.

## Part two: Pakhowal Road out to Daad

| Road | About |
|---|---|
| Pakhowal Road | 2.0 km |
| Jodhan - Ludhiana Road (outer Pakhowal Road) | 2.8 km |

The second half is simpler. You stay on Pakhowal Road for about 2 km, and then OpenStreetMap starts calling it "Jodhan - Ludhiana Road". It is still Pakhowal Road to locals and in our address. There are no more changes of road in our route data until you reach us.

The seven named stretches total about 8.4 km. OSRM's 9.5 km also counts short junction links that it doesn't list by name.

## Names to give a driver

If someone else is driving, or you are directing a guest over the phone, a short chain of names does the job. Start with Bharat Nagar Chowk, then Ferozepur Road, then Pakhowal Road. Hero Bakery Chowk and Krishna Mandir Road are the two short links in the middle. After that it is Pakhowal Road all the way out to Daad, where the last names are Omaxe Flats and Cerajot Tiles. Read in that order, they match OSRM's route from start to finish.

## Is 14 minutes realistic?

Only on empty roads. OSRM's estimate leaves out traffic entirely, and part one runs through a string of city junctions. Allow extra time at peak hours. If you are heading out after work on a weekday for a dinner booking, add a generous margin.

## Starting from the railway station instead

The railway station route joins this one early. From Ludhiana Junction, OSRM takes Ferozepur Road for about 0.8 km and then follows the same five roads that come after Ferozepur Road on this page, for 10.7 km in total. The full turn list is on our [railway station directions](../from-railway-station/).

## Finding Red Wood Farm on the outer stretch

On the 2.8 km stretch near Daad, look for these in turn:

- Omaxe Flats (Omaxe Royal Residency), about 0.8 km from us by road
- Daad village, written "Dad" on OpenStreetMap, under a kilometer from our pin
- Cerajot Tiles, which faces us across the road

Red Wood Farm is opposite Cerajot Tiles. Turn in at our garden gate and you will see the green hedge arch with flowers. In the evening the palms along the driveway are lit with fairy lights.

We should be clear about access: Red Wood Farm has no wheelchair access. Please keep that in mind when planning a visit with someone who uses a wheelchair.

## Getting here without driving

A cab or an auto booked on an app will get you here from anywhere on Ferozepur Road. Set Red Wood Farm on Pakhowal Road as the destination, then check the pin. Before you leave, message us at 096467 13555 and ask us to share our location. Forward that pin to the driver so the outer stretch holds no surprises.
