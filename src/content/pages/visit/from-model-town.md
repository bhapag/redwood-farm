Model Town is about 6.9 km from Red Wood Farm by road. Take Model Town Road and Krishna Mandir Road onto Pakhowal Road, then stay on Pakhowal Road as it heads out of the city to Daad. OSRM estimates about 11 minutes on clear roads. Expect the real drive to take longer at busy times.

## Step by step from Model Town

This is the route OSRM produced on 17 Nov 2026, with road names as OpenStreetMap shows them.

**Step 1. Model Town Road, about 0.6 km.** OSRM's starting point sits on Model Town Road, in the Gurudev Nagar part of the map. Head along it to the first turn.

**Step 2. Krishna Mandir Road, about 0.5 km.** A short connector. It is the same link used by the routes from the railway station, the ISBT bus stand and Ferozepur Road, so you will see it on several of our route pages.

**Step 3. Pakhowal Road, about 2.0 km.** You are now on Pakhowal Road proper. Keep going away from the city center.

**Step 4. Jodhan - Ludhiana Road, about 2.8 km.** OpenStreetMap changes the label here, but it is the same road you were on. Locals and our address call it Pakhowal Road. This is the final leg.

Add up the named roads and you get about 5.9 km. The remaining 1.0 km or so is streets in Model Town and short pieces at junctions, which OSRM counts in its 6.9 km total without naming. Your own house may sit a little closer or farther than OSRM's start point.

## How long it really takes

OSRM's 11 minutes is a no-traffic figure. It doesn't account for signals, queues or a festival evening. Treat it as the shortest the trip could be. On a weekday at peak hours, allow extra time, and more again if you have guests arriving for a party and want to be there first.

For a sense of scale, Model Town is roughly level with Sarabha Nagar in our route data: 6.9 km and 6.8 km, both about 11 minutes. [Dugri](../from-dugri/) is a little shorter at 6.5 km.

## Coming from the ISBT bus stand instead

Friends arriving by bus can follow almost the same route. ISBT Ludhiana is also listed in Gurudev Nagar, and OSRM routes it along Krishna Mandir Road (about 0.5 km), Pakhowal Road (about 2.0 km) and Jodhan - Ludhiana Road (about 2.8 km). The total is about 8.2 km, roughly 13 minutes with no traffic. If you are collecting someone off a bus, pick them up first and the rest of the drive is shared.

## What you'll see near the end

On the last 2.8 km you reach the area around Daad. These are the landmarks in our address:

- **Daad village**, spelled "Dad" on OpenStreetMap, under a kilometer from our pin.
- **Omaxe Flats**, the Omaxe Royal Residency development, about 0.8 km from us by road.
- **Cerajot Tiles**, across the road from Red Wood Farm.

Pull in at the garden gate opposite Cerajot Tiles. The green hedge arch with flowers is the entrance, and at night the palms along the driveway carry fairy lights.

One thing to know in advance: there is no wheelchair access at Red Wood Farm. Families with a wheelchair user should plan for that.

## Without a car

From Model Town, a cab or an auto booked through an app is the simplest choice. Set the drop point to Red Wood Farm on Pakhowal Road. To be sure the driver stops at the right gate, ask us on 096467 13555 to share our location and forward it to them. That works better than reading out directions over the phone.

## Why people from Model Town make the drive

The drive out to Daad brings you to open lawn, tables under palm trees and string lights in the evening. Guests have described it as feeling "close to nature" (gurvinder kaur, Google review). The food is North Indian, tandoor and Indo-Chinese, and our house dishes include the Atta Chicken (₹925), chicken cooked sealed inside wheat-flour dough.
