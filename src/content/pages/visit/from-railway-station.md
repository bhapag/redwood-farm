Ludhiana Railway Station is about 10.7 km from Red Wood Farm by road. You leave on Ferozepur Road, switch to Pakhowal Road, and stay with it as it runs out of the city towards Daad. OSRM, an open routing engine built on OpenStreetMap data, puts the drive at about 16 minutes on empty roads. Real traffic adds to that.

## Which roads does the route use, in order?

This route comes from OSRM, checked on 17 Nov 2026. The road names are the ones OpenStreetMap uses, so a signboard may spell a few of them differently.

1. **Ferozepur Road, about 0.8 km.** You leave the station area and join Ferozepur Road.
2. **Pakhowal Road, about 1.3 km.** The route turns off Ferozepur Road onto Pakhowal Road.
3. **Satpal Mittal Road, about 0.3 km.** A short link. Our route data places Hero Bakery Chowk on this road, which is a handy name to give a driver.
4. **Krishna Mandir Road, about 0.5 km.** Another short connector that brings you back onto Pakhowal Road.
5. **Pakhowal Road, about 2.0 km.** The main run out of the built-up part of the city.
6. **Jodhan - Ludhiana Road, about 2.8 km.** This is the outer stretch of Pakhowal Road. OpenStreetMap labels it with this name. Our address, and most people you ask, still call it Pakhowal Road.

Those six named stretches add up to about 7.7 km. The other 3 km or so is made of short turns and junction links that OSRM counts in the total without giving each one a name. Steps 3 and 4 come close together, so set your navigation before you pull away from the station.

## How long should you allow?

Read OSRM's 16 minutes as the best case. It assumes no traffic at all, which you will rarely get near a busy railway station. Signals, crowds outside the station after a train arrives and slow traffic on the inner roads all stretch the trip. Allow extra time at peak hours and on festival evenings. If you are heading back for a train after your meal, leave Red Wood Farm earlier than the map suggests.

## Getting here by cab or auto

Most people arriving by train will book a cab or an auto on a ride-hailing app. Type "Red Wood Farm, Pakhowal Road" as the drop point and look at the pin on the map before you confirm the ride. Many drivers will know Pakhowal Road by name. Fewer will know exactly where it reaches Daad, so keep three landmarks ready: Daad village, Cerajot Tiles and Omaxe Flats.

We can't quote a fare. It depends on the app, the vehicle and the time of day.

### Send "share location" first

Before you leave the platform, message us at 096467 13555 and ask us to share our location. Open the pin in your maps app, or forward it straight to your driver. It saves a roadside phone call and points the car at the right gate.

## Driving your own car

Once you are on Pakhowal Road the drive is simple: you keep going until the city thins out near Daad. The early part, up to the point where you rejoin Pakhowal Road, takes the most attention, because the route switches roads four times in under 3 km of named road. After that, the last 4.8 km is two long stretches with no change of road in our route data.

## Finding the gate at the end

The final 2.8 km is the stretch OpenStreetMap labels Jodhan - Ludhiana Road. As you get close, Daad village sits under a kilometer from our pin and Omaxe Flats, also known as Omaxe Royal Residency, is just off the road. Red Wood Farm stands opposite Cerajot Tiles. Turn in at the garden gate and walk through the green hedge arch with its flowers. After dark the palms along our driveway are wrapped in fairy lights, which tells you that you have the right place.

A note for families travelling with an older relative or a wheelchair user: Red Wood Farm has no wheelchair access. Please plan for that before you set out.
