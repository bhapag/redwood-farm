From Sarabha Nagar, Red Wood Farm is about 6.8 km by road and takes about 11 minutes when the roads are clear. You need only three roads: a short link that OpenStreetMap calls Rotary Club, then Pakhowal Road, then its outer stretch towards Daad. We are opposite Cerajot Tiles, near Omaxe Flats.

## The route at a glance

| Order | Road (as named on OpenStreetMap) | About |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rotary Club | 0.8 km |
| 2 | Pakhowal Road | 2.0 km |
| 3 | Jodhan - Ludhiana Road (outer Pakhowal Road) | 2.8 km |
| | **Total by road** | **6.8 km** |

Source: OSRM route, checked on 17 Nov 2026. The three named roads cover about 5.6 km. The remaining 1.2 km is lanes inside Sarabha Nagar and short junction links, which OSRM includes in the total but doesn't name.

## Where does the route start?

OSRM measured from the point OpenStreetMap uses as the center of Sarabha Nagar. Your own block may be a few hundred meters closer or farther, so the total for you could differ by a little. The order of roads stays the same for most of the neighborhood, because every route out of here meets Pakhowal Road early.

## Leg by leg

### Rotary Club, about 0.8 km

"Rotary Club" is the label OpenStreetMap gives this first stretch. It is an unusual name for a road and you may not see it on a signboard, so follow your navigation for this part. It is the shortest leg and it ends where you join Pakhowal Road.

### Pakhowal Road, about 2.0 km

This is the inner part of Pakhowal Road. Traffic here is city traffic, with junctions and signals. Stay on it and keep heading away from the city.

### Outer Pakhowal Road, about 2.8 km

OpenStreetMap switches the name to "Jodhan - Ludhiana Road" for this last stretch. Locals and our own address still call it Pakhowal Road. You are on the right road as long as you haven't turned off since the previous leg.

## How accurate is the 11-minute estimate?

OSRM calculates time with no traffic at all. On a Sunday morning you may come close to it. On a weekday evening, or the night of a big festival, expect it to take longer. Allow extra time at peak hours, especially if you have a table booked or guests waiting at a party.

Sarabha Nagar sits among the closer neighborhoods in our route data. For comparison, OSRM gives the same 11 minutes from [Model Town](../from-model-town/) at 6.9 km, and 12 minutes from Dugri at 6.5 km.

## Spotting Red Wood Farm on the last stretch

Three markers tell you that you are almost there:

- **Omaxe Flats**, also called Omaxe Royal Residency, just off the road. OSRM puts it about 0.8 km from us by road.
- **Daad village**, which OpenStreetMap spells "Dad", under a kilometer from our pin.
- **Cerajot Tiles**, directly across the road from us.

When you reach Cerajot Tiles, Red Wood Farm is on the opposite side. Turn in at the garden gate. You will pass the green hedge arch with flowers, and in the evening the lamp-lit stone path takes you into the grounds.

Red Wood Farm does not have wheelchair access, so please plan ahead if someone in your group uses a wheelchair.

## Heading back to Sarabha Nagar

The return trip normally retraces the same three roads: outer Pakhowal Road, then inner Pakhowal Road, then the short link into Sarabha Nagar. Your maps app may choose a different line through a junction or two at night, which is fine. We close at 11:00 PM, so if you are booking a late dinner, plan the drive home with that in mind. If you came by cab, book the ride back from our gate and give the driver "opposite Cerajot Tiles, near Omaxe Flats" as the pickup, so they stop on the right side of the road.
