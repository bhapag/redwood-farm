Pakhowal Road is one of the roads that runs from Ludhiana city outwards, past the built-up neighborhoods and on towards Daad village. Routes from Ferozepur Road, Model Town, Sarabha Nagar and the railway station all feed into it. Near Daad, OpenStreetMap labels its outer stretch "Jodhan - Ludhiana Road". Red Wood Farm sits on that stretch.

This guide pieces the road together from real route data: OSRM routes over OpenStreetMap, checked on 17 Nov 2026. We describe only what that data shows, so treat it as a map reader's guide rather than a local history.

## Where is Pakhowal Road in Ludhiana?

Think of it as a road with an inner city part and an outer part.

**The inner part** is where the city's roads meet it. In our route data:

- From **Ferozepur Road**, the routes from the railway station and from Bharat Nagar Chowk turn onto Pakhowal Road after a short run of under 1.5 km on Ferozepur Road.
- From **Sarabha Nagar**, a short stretch that OpenStreetMap calls "Rotary Club" leads onto it.
- From **Model Town** and the **ISBT bus stand**, Krishna Mandir Road connects to it.
- Near **Hero Bakery Chowk** on Satpal Mittal Road, routes step off Pakhowal Road and rejoin it through Satpal Mittal Road and Krishna Mandir Road.

**The outer part** begins where OpenStreetMap switches the name. Every city route we checked ends with the same 2.8 km on "Jodhan - Ludhiana Road" before reaching Red Wood Farm. Around here, OpenStreetMap shows villages such as Daad, Thakarwal, Lalton Kalan and Threeke, along with residential areas like Palam Vihaar, Omaxe Royal Residency and Basant Avenue.

## Why the map shows two names

You will find the same road under two names. Signboards, addresses and conversation use "Pakhowal Road". OpenStreetMap, and apps built on it, may show "Jodhan - Ludhiana Road" for the outer stretch near Daad. Both lead to the same place. If your maps app shows the second name when you are looking for the first, you are on the right road.

There is a similar quirk with the village. Our address says Daad. OpenStreetMap spells it "Dad". It is the same village, about 0.8 km from our pin in a straight line.

## Landmarks on the outer stretch

When you reach the Daad end of the road, three names from our address help you place yourself:

1. **Omaxe Flats**, the Omaxe Royal Residency development. OSRM lists it on Thakarwal Link Road, about 0.8 km from us by road.
2. **Daad village.** OSRM's route from the village uses Dad Link Road for about 0.3 km before joining Jodhan - Ludhiana Road.
3. **Cerajot Tiles.** Red Wood Farm is directly opposite.

Our gate opens off the road opposite Cerajot Tiles. Inside you will find a green hedge arch with flowers, a "Red Wood Farm" name arch, and garden paths lit by lamp posts at night.

## What Red Wood Farm is, for anyone new to this end of the road

Red Wood Farm is a garden family restaurant first and a party lawn second. We serve North Indian, tandoor and Indo-Chinese food from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM, every day, on an open lawn with tables under palm trees and string lights. Takeaway can be ordered by phone or message. On Google we had a rating of 4.2 from 72 reviews when we checked on 14 Nov 2026.

There is no wheelchair access at Red Wood Farm, which matters if you are planning a visit for someone who uses a wheelchair.
