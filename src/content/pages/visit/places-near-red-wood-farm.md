According to OpenStreetMap, the closest named places to Red Wood Farm are Daad village (about 0.8 km), Palam Vihaar (0.9 km) and Thakarwal village (1.1 km). Gurdwara Bhai Bala Sahib Ji is about 1.6 km away, and Basant Avenue about 3 km. All distances here are straight lines from our map pin.

## How we made this list

We pulled the places OpenStreetMap lists within about 3 km of Red Wood Farm on 17 Nov 2026 and kept them as they came back. We haven't visited each place, and we have added nothing from memory.

Two things to keep in mind:

- **Distances are straight lines.** Roads bend, so the drive is longer. Daad village, for example, is 0.8 km from our pin on the map but about 1.2 km by road, according to OSRM.
- **OpenStreetMap is volunteer-edited.** A shop may have closed or a clinic may have moved. Call ahead before you make a special trip to anything listed here.

Some entries have no name on the map, only a type such as "Temple" or "Park". We list those as the map shows them.
